## Promise (buyer-facing)

**Studio Sequence** is a mid-page (or hero) **cinematic camera pull-out**. Visitors begin *inside* your film - full bleed, no street, no frame. As they scroll, the world draws back around the billboard until that same story is still playing on a facade in a living street.

**No Scroller (pin-until-complete).** Scroll aims the camera. The page does not physically scroll during the viewing. The film free-plays on its own clock. When the journey ends, the pin **releases**. Then the **page owns the wheel** until the stage docks at the top of the viewport again. Pointer on the next sibling never drives the camera. Not a tall sticky track. Not GSAP ScrollTrigger. Not Lenis. Not PSAVE. Do not rewind the film.

**Why use it:**
- Your film becomes architecture, not a flat clip in a box
- Drop in campaign, manifesto, product, or brand cinema - the stage adapts
- The story plays through uninterrupted while scroll only moves the camera
- Visitors feel a living billboard, calm and expensive
- One elegant section - after a hero or as the open itself

Default demo content (Surreal Studio film, NY street plate) is only a starting board. Tell your AI to restage film, plate, and board measure until it reads as you alone.

**How you build it:** download the product pack (prompt + source + plate + billboard film). Tell your AI: *Build Studio Sequence using the files in this folder. Read PROMPT.md and follow it. Prefer source/StudioSequence.tsx and source/studio-data.ts. No Scroller: pin-until-complete. One 100dvh stage. Scroll aims virtual progress on 4 viewports desktop / 3 viewports mobile. Camera follows that progress 1:1. Film free-plays. At 0 plus up, or 1 plus down, release so the host page can continue. After release at the end, the page owns scroll until the stage docks (top >= 0; implement >= -2). Do not install gsap. Do not install lenis. Do not build a tall multi-vh sticky track. Do not add PSAVE. Do not seek the film with scroll.*

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** **No Scroller** = **pin-until-complete**. Camera is driven by **virtual progress** `g` 0→1. Film free-plays. Not PSAVE.

```
Earn desktop: VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS = 4
Earn mobile (<768): VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS = 3
virtualDistance = VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS * window.innerHeight
delta progress = wheelDeltaY / virtualDistance
```

Gestures map **1:1** onto that distance. No wheel gain. No GSAP lag. Camera follows `g` immediately.

| Event | Timing | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Pin engage | section in view, journey not finished | consume wheel / touch / keys |
| Hold in | g < 0.06 | stay full-bleed |
| Pull-out | 0.06 → 0.90 | smootherstep scale startScale → 1 |
| Hold out | g > 0.90 | street settled |
| Film time | independent | start to end, then loop. Never seek |
| Release | g ≤ 0 + up, or g ≥ 1 + down | host page may continue |
| **Pin freeing** | after g = 1 + down | **page owns** until stage docks (`top >= -2`) |
| Pointer on next sibling | never | runway never drives the camera |

**Hard ban:** GSAP `ScrollTrigger` pin + `scrub`.
**Hard ban:** Lenis / SmoothScroll.
**Hard ban:** a 3 vh / 4 vh **document spacer** as the method (those numbers are **virtual earn** only).
**Hard ban:** installing `gsap` or `lenis` for this section.
**Hard ban:** PSAVE / GOP 3 / reverse film. Studio film free-plays.
**Hard ban:** mapping scroll to `video.currentTime`.
**Hard ban:** `overflow: hidden` on the host page (release must be able to reveal the next sibling).

Root marker: `data-studio-drive="pin"` · `data-product="MS-SEC-STUDIO01"`. After release: `data-studio-owns="page"`. While the pin owns: `data-studio-owns="pin"`. Capture helper: `window.__msScrollNarrative = { setProgress, getProgress, getTarget, pageOwns, productId: "MS-SEC-STUDIO01" }`.

**Confirm (desktop 900px tall):** two 1800px flicks on 4 viewports = `g = 1.0` with page `scrollY` still 0. One more down-scroll releases. First up after release moves the page, not the camera.

**Reduced motion:** world scale holds at 1 (street rest). Film may still play.
