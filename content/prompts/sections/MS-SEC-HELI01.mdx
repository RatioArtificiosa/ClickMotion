## Promise (buyer-facing)

HELIX is a mid-page **gallery carousel section** that turns scroll into a spatial presentation. Nine of **your** design pieces ride a WebGL cylindrical helix while giant titles cross the frame and a quiet center lockup holds the brand. Visitors do not skim a flat grid. They move through a crafted arc of work.

**No Scroller (pin-until-complete).** Scroll aims the helix. The page does not physically scroll during the viewing. When the journey ends, the pin **releases**. Then the **page owns the wheel** until the stage docks at the top of the viewport again. Pointer on the next sibling never drives the helix. Not a tall sticky track. Not GSAP ScrollTrigger. Not Lenis. Not PSAVE. There is no reverse-played film.

**Why use it:**
- Your portfolio or case studies feel like a private viewing, not a stock carousel
- Scroll owns the motion, so the section stays intentional and calm under the user's hand
- Every image, title, wordmark, body line, and stage color is meant to be replaced with your brand system
- Drop-in section (not a full homepage), so it sits cleanly after a hero or before contact
- Built for design studios, creative agencies, and brands that sell craft with space and pacing

Default demo content (ClickMotion wordmark, sample mock cards, "Design in / motion") is only a starting board. Tell your AI to restage everything until it reads as you alone.

**How you build it:** download the product pack (prompt + source + nine stills). Tell your AI: *Build HELIX using the files in this folder. Read PROMPT.md and follow it. Prefer source/HelixGallerySection.tsx and source/OrbitHelix.tsx. No Scroller: pin-until-complete. One 100dvh stage. Scroll aims virtual progress on 5 viewports desktop / 3 viewports mobile. Titles and helix follow that progress 1:1. At 0 plus up, or 1 plus down, release so the host page can continue. After release at the end, the page owns scroll until the stage docks (top >= 0; implement >= -2). Do not install gsap. Do not install lenis. Do not build a tall multi-vh sticky track. Do not add PSAVE. There is no film to rewind.*

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** **No Scroller** = **pin-until-complete**. Cards and titles are driven by **virtual progress** `g` 0→1, not a free-play film, not a tall page scrollbar.

```
Earn desktop: VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS = 5
Earn mobile (<768): VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS = 3
virtualDistance = VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS * window.innerHeight
delta progress = wheelDeltaY / virtualDistance
```

Gestures map **1:1** onto that distance. No wheel gain. No GSAP lag. Old `scrub: true` feel: titles and helix follow `g` immediately.

| Event | Timing | Ease / notes |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Pin engage | section in view, journey not finished | consume wheel / touch / keys |
| Helix phase `g` | 0→1 over the earn track | maps card path offset |
| Title A | g maps x from `-100vw` to `+100vw` | peak center ~g=0.18 |
| Title B | g maps x from `+100vw` to `-100vw` | mirror of A |
| Center lockup fade | after g≈0.55 | opacity falls toward 0 by ~g=0.8 |
| Cards enter | g from 0 | ease in from off-path (no seeded mid-arc) |
| Rails draw | with P = min(1, g/(400/600)) | guide lines grow with helix |
| Release | g ≤ 0 + up, or g ≥ 1 + down | host page may continue |
| **Pin freeing** | after g = 1 + down | **page owns** until stage docks (`top >= -2`) |
| Pointer on next sibling | never | runway never drives the helix |

**Hard ban:** GSAP `ScrollTrigger` pin + `scrub`.
**Hard ban:** Lenis coupled to ScrollTrigger.
**Hard ban:** a 3 vh / 5 vh **document spacer** as the method (those numbers are **virtual earn** only).
**Hard ban:** installing `gsap` or `lenis` for this section.
**Hard ban:** PSAVE / GOP 3 / reverse film. Helix has no film clock.
**Hard ban:** `overflow: hidden` on the host page (release must be able to reveal the next sibling).

Root marker: `data-helix-drive="pin"`. After release: `data-helix-owns="page"`. While the pin owns: `data-helix-owns="pin"`. Capture helper: `window.__msScrollNarrative = { setProgress, getProgress, getTarget, pageOwns, productId: "MS-SEC-HELI01" }`.

**Reduced motion:** show static mid pose (g≈0.45), titles near center, no chase.
