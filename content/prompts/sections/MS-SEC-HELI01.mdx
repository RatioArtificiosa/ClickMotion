## Promise (buyer-facing)

HELIX is a mid-page **gallery carousel section** that turns scroll into a spatial presentation. Nine of **your** design pieces ride a WebGL cylindrical helix while giant titles cross the frame and a quiet center lockup holds the brand. Visitors do not skim a flat grid. They move through a crafted arc of work.

**Why use it:**
- Your portfolio or case studies feel like a private viewing, not a stock carousel
- Scroll owns the motion, so the section stays intentional and calm under the user's hand
- Every image, title, wordmark, body line, and stage color is meant to be replaced with your brand system
- Drop-in section (not a full homepage), so it sits cleanly after a hero or before contact
- Built for design studios, creative agencies, and brands that sell craft with space and pacing

Default demo content (ClickMotion wordmark, sample mock cards, "Design in / motion") is only a starting board. Tell your AI to restage everything until it reads as you alone.

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **HELIX** - mid-page **carousel / gallery section** (not a full-bleed hero). Cool gray stage `#C3C3C3` by default (fully recolorable). Nine design stills ride a WebGL cylindrical helix while giant type **"Design in"** and **"motion"** cross the frame. Center lockup: brand wordmark (Birthstone or your script) above a quiet uppercase line. Stage is solid color by default so **your cards own the focus**. Scroll owns the pin.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** editorial Dribbble showcase × Swiss gray board × private-studio restraint. Serious. Spatial. Readable under cards.

**Typography:**
- Giant titles: geometric sans (Neue Haas / Inter / Helvetica Neue), uppercase, `clamp(3.25rem, 13vw, 9.5rem)`, tracking `-0.04em`, line-height `0.8`, ink `#1a1a1a`
- Wordmark: **Birthstone** (Google Fonts) or your brand script, `clamp(1.85rem, 3.6vw, 2.75rem)`, near-black on light stage (no white glow on light gray)
- Center line: 12-13px uppercase tracking `0.08em`
- Concepts body: 13-14px, max-width ~16rem, bottom-left, opacity ~0.8

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#C3C3C3` | Your board color (keep type readable) |
| Ink | `#0a0a0a` / `#1a1a1a` | Your primary type |
| Rails | `#9E9E9E` ~50% | Soft guide lines on stage |
| Pin spacer | match stage | Never flash white under pin |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** purple mesh, emoji, rainbow H1, Motionsites pill docks, required third-party Dribbble CTA, footer stripe wipe as the product signature, auto-rotating carousel without scroll pin, leaving demo ClickMotion copy if the buyer brand is different.

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** scroll-scrub pin (GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis). Cards and titles are driven by scroll progress, not a free-play film.

| Event | Timing | Ease / notes |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Pin engage | start `top top` | pinType fixed, scrub true |
| Helix phase `g` | 0→1 over full pin | maps card path offset |
| Title A | g maps x from `-100vw` to `+100vw` | peak center ~g=0.18 |
| Title B | g maps x from `+100vw` to `-100vw` | mirror of A |
| Center lockup fade | after g≈0.55 | opacity falls toward 0 by ~g=0.8 |
| Cards enter | g from 0 | ease in from off-path (no seeded mid-arc) |
| Rails draw | with P = min(1, g/(400/600)) | guide lines grow with helix |

**Reduced motion:** show static mid pose (g≈0.45), titles near center, no long scrub track required.

## Media Integration (gallery stills - fully yours)

This section is a **card gallery**, not a full-bleed video hero. The default pack ships nine stills:

```
/assets/images/orbit/orbit-01.jpg … orbit-09.jpg
```

Load order on the helix is **reversed** (09 → 01). Each card is a rounded-rect shader plane on a helical ribbon (radius 12, pitch climb 28 over 2 turns, spacing 6.2).

**What buyers put on the cards:** product shots, UI frames, campaign boards, editorial photography, case-study covers. Prefer portrait-ish boards (rough aspect near 568/812). High resolution. No watermarks. Consistent lighting reads best as a set.

**Optional stage treatment:** keep solid stage color (recommended for focus), or ask your AI to add a very soft texture/gradient behind cards if your brand needs it. Do not bury the cards under a loud background film unless you deliberately want that.

## Accessibility

- Semantic `<section>` + heading hierarchy
- Wordmark `aria-label` with the spoken brand name
- Canvas `aria-hidden`
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no long scrub required
- Contrast: dark type on light stage (or invert if you recolor)

## Performance Notes

- R3F Canvas `dpr` capped at 2; transparent clear
- Textures: LinearFilter, no mipmaps, SRGB
- Dispose geos/mats/textures on unmount
- Lenis only on the shell that owns the pin
- Do not load drei unless you add controls later
