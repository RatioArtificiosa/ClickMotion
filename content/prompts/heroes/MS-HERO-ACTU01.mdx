## Promise (buyer-facing)

**Actually!** is a full-viewport **product hero** that treats the vessel as the brand. A living 3D can drops into a quiet editorial stage; a pointer-driven circular window peels bone paper into ink studio light; scroll pins the frame, expands the reveal, and opens support copy about the formula. Visitors do not watch a stock film. They meet the product.

**Why use it:**
- Your can, bottle, or vessel feels premium and touchable on first paint
- Pointer and grab turn the hero into a private tasting of craft, not a flat render
- Scroll owns the second act so the formula story lands with intention
- Every string, label texture, mesh, and color token is meant to be replaced with your brand system
- Built for CPG, beverage, wellness drinks, and physical products that sell with restraint

Default demo content (ACTUALLY. wordmark, nootropic formula, Clear SKU) is only a starting board. Tell your AI to restage everything until it reads as you alone.

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **Actually!** - full-bleed **product can hero** (not a mid-page gallery). Bone paper `#efede6` over ink `#1a1b1d`. Giant wordmark **ACTUALLY.** (buyer rebrands). Studio HDRI + labeled GLB can. Circle clip-path window follows the pointer; scroll expands the window, locks the can, and reveals left support copy. Soft clear accent `#bcd3d8`. Editorial CPG restraint. Not SaaS glass. Not purple mesh.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** premium beverage launch × Swiss editorial type × tactile product photography - digital. Serious. Quiet. Readable.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: heavy geometric sans (Inter Black / Söhne Breit stand-in), `19vw` desktop, tracking `-0.03em`, ink on bone; accent period in clear
- Support H2: light display serif (Georgia), `clamp(28px, 2.8vw, 44px)`, bone on ink
- Meta / location: 11px uppercase tracking `0.24em`, mist
- Body: 16px, leading 1.65, bone/80, max ~42ch

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**

**Anti-slop hard ban:** purple mesh, emoji, rainbow H1, Motionsites pill docks, free-play background film as the product signature, auto-rotating carousel without scroll pin, leaving demo ACTUALLY. copy if the buyer brand is different, stock Unsplash can as the only 3D.

## Layout Structure

- **Hero only** - no forced site chrome inside the component
- **Desktop stack:** bone paper layer (wordmark + taglines + scroll hint) over ink stage with clip-path circle window into WebGL can + support column
- **Pin:** start `top top`, end `+=120%`, scrub true
- **Mobile:** stacked wordmark, can height ~52vh, drag-to-spin, formula block below
- **Loader:** bone curtain, FLIP into hero H1, soft ~2.2s / hard 9s

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** scroll-scrub pin + pointer window + 3D grab. Not free-play film.

| Event | Timing | Ease / notes |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Loader | soft 2.2s | bone curtain → reveal |
| Can entrance | ~1.6s after reveal | drop + spin ease-out |
| Clip entrance | 1.2s delay 0.15 | circle radius grows |
| Pointer follow | continuous | quickTo x/y on fine pointer |
| Pin scrub | +=120% | expand clip, lock blend, dolly |
| Support reveal | pin progress > ~0.58 | stagger fade-up |
| Grab drag | any time (pre-lock) | pointer capture on can |

## Technical implementation notes for AI tools

Build a single default-export React component `ActuallyHero` (or page section).

Stack: React + TypeScript, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis coupled to ScrollTrigger.update, three + @react-three/fiber + @react-three/drei (`Environment`, `ContactShadows`, `useGLTF`).

Canvas: transparent, ACES tone mapping, studio lights + HDRI `background={false}`, dpr cap ~1.5.

Desktop clip-path circle on the ink layer; bone paper sibling with wordmark. Mobile skips clip-path pin; keep drag spin.

`prefers-reduced-motion`: skip long pin scrub, show static product pose and readable copy.

## Performance Notes

- R3F Canvas `dpr` capped (~1.5 hero); ACES tone mapping
- `useGLTF.preload` for the can mesh
- Dispose label textures and materials on unmount
- HDRI Environment with `background={false}`
- Lenis only on the shell that owns the pin
- ContactShadows resolution modest (512)
