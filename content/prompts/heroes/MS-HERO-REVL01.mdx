## Promise (buyer-facing)

**REVEL** is a fashion-commerce **pin-until-complete** scroll hero: one full-viewport pearl stage, a 20-second iPhone breakout film the playhead walks through four chapters from feed to freedom. **PSAVE** (Perfect Scroll Video Engine): scroll aims, down plays forward, up plays reverse, the picture never jumps a frame. Not a tall multi-page scrollbar track. Not Meridian dark coastal. Not Vertex mono security.

**How you build it:** this listing is **PDF-only** (no files zip). Give your AI this sold prompt plus the Revel package PDF. Tell it: *Build REVEL with PSAVE (Perfect Scroll Video Engine). Pin-until-complete. Scroll aims on 12 viewports so the kick is earned (about 5 or 6 scrolls to halfway). Down-scroll plays the film forward at 1.2x. Up-scroll plays it backward at the same 1.2x, one 3-frame step per seek. Never jump a frame. When they lift, leftover dest plus a 0.55s dest floor keeps the film going a little, then rate eases to a stop. Copy and the rose bar follow the picture. Release only when the picture arrives. After release the page owns the atelier. Replacement films must be re-encoded GOP 3, no B-frames. Do not seek the playhead across the film. Do not copy old Vertex seek-scrub or the old Revel wheel-gain scrub. Do not build a 480vh sticky track.*

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** Scroll-as-narrative · **pin-until-complete** · **PSAVE** (Perfect Scroll Video Engine). Scroll sets a destination on `12` viewports. Down plays the film forward at **1.2x**. Up plays it backward at the same **1.2x**, **exactly one 3-frame step per seek**. Never jump a frame. **When they lift, leftover dest plus a 0.55s dest floor keeps the film going a little**, then rate eases to a stop. **Do not** autoplay-loop as primary. **Do not** seek `currentTime` across a jump.

**Hard ban:** tall multi-vh document scrollbar track (legacy `480vh` sticky pattern).  
**Hard ban:** seeking the playhead to a far destination (GSAP scrub, wheel-gain dump, old Vertex `0.22`, old Revel `0.11` / GSAP lag `0.55` / swipe `0.09`). Live Vertex is PSAVE (`3.6` + 0.55 coast), not that scrub.  
**Hard ban:** a 3.8 or 7.2 aim track on this 20s film (two flicks dump dest; feels like a video). Locked earn is **12**.  
**Hard ban:** a low-res frame buffer or canvas overlay for reverse. Reverse walks the live video.  
**Hard ban:** releasing the pin because the destination hit 1 while the picture is still walking.  
**Hard ban:** a window wheel listener that keeps driving the film when the pointer is on the atelier.  
**Hard ban:** a replacement film with one I-frame (or long GOP + B-frames). Reverse will stall mid-film.  
**Hard ban:** pausing on the last wheel tick. The stop is friction, then a graceful slowdown.

```
const VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS = 12;  // GOLD: earned. Not Elyse 3.6. Not old 3.8 seek-scrub.
const PSAVE_RATE = 1.2;        // film-seconds per wall-second, both directions
const PSAVE_FRAME = 1 / 24;    // never skip more than one frame in a forward tick
const PSAVE_REV_STRIDE = 3;    // reverse: exactly 3 frames per seek (0.125s)
const PSAVE_LIVE_MS = 280;     // still scrolling if last up/down is inside this window
const PSAVE_COAST_SEC = 0.55;  // leftover dest floor after last real intent
const PSAVE_EASE_SEC = 0.55;   // forward rate tapers over last leftover film-seconds
const PSAVE_FLIP_DEADZONE_PX = 32; // ignore tiny opposite trackpad ticks
```

**Perceived time:** this film is designed slow then fast. She lives in the phone and walks up to the glass (slow). Then a kick and jump out (fast). Halfway is “she leaves the viewpoint.” That beat takes about **5 or 6 scrolls**. Do not remaster the film to flatten the cut. Size the aim track so the kick is earned.

**The lift:** leftover dest is the Elyse stop. On this 20s film dest often dies (1.2x eats it; a tiny opposite tick snaps dest). After last real intent, dest must sit at least `0.55s` of film ahead. Ignore opposite ticks under `32px`. Forward `playbackRate` stays 1.2 until leftover dest is under `0.55s`, then tapers toward ~0.42. Friction, then a graceful stop. This `0.55` is **not** old GSAP scrub lag.

**Rebuild algorithm (mandatory):** one `100dvh` stage in **normal document flow**. `#atelier` is the **next sibling**. Do not `overflow: hidden` the page. Do not import ScrollTrigger or GSAP scrub. Do not use `position: sticky`. Do not build a `480vh` spacer. Gestures map **1:1** onto `12` viewports and only move the **destination**. First **real** opposite gesture snaps destination to the picture. Ignore tiny opposite ticks. Each frame, walk the picture toward that destination at **1.2x**, never more than **1/24s** of film on a forward fallback tick. **Down:** muted `play()` at `playbackRate 1.2`, ease over the last `0.55s` of leftover dest. After they lift, apply the `0.55s` dest floor once if dest is closer than that. **Up:** first real up-scroll cancels a pending forward destination. Walk `currentTime` backward exactly one 3-frame step per seek, **during the gesture and after**. Wait `seeked`. Never seek to the stop point. No loop. No low-res buffer. Copy, chapters, and the rose bar follow the **playhead**. Release only when the **picture** is at `0+up` / `1+down`. Test live `currentTime` as well as the playhead ref (settle can stop ~1 frame short). After release the **page** owns scroll until the stage docks at the top. Pointer on `#atelier` never drives the film. **Opening frame:** the poster is frame 0 (gold phone / feed). Kick-seek `0.04 → 0`, wait `seeked`, fade the film in. Reduced motion may keep that poster and seek to `~0.45`.

| Element | Motion |
|---------|--------|
| Film | PSAVE: play toward destination forward or reverse, never jump a frame |
| Stage | Pinned 100dvh; no tall page track |
| Wheel | 1:1 onto the 12 aim track (destination only) |
| Lift | 0.55s dest floor + rate ease. Friction, then a graceful stop. |
| Chapters | Crossfade eyebrow/title/body from **playhead** (revelFade / revelRise) |
| Progress bar | `scaleX(playhead)` rose-gold gradient |
| Scroll cue | visible only when playhead &lt; 0.05 |
| Reduced motion | 100dvh static stage, mid-film still (~0.45), no PSAVE |
