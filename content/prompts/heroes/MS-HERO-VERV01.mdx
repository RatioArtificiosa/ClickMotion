## Promise (buyer-facing)

**VERVE SOCIAL** is a full-viewport **creator social platform hero**: plum-ink night, hot rose + amber signal, bold Clash/Syne lockup (**BE PRESENT. / BE TOGETHER.**), a living culture film of human presence, and an infinite marquee of community energy. Film free-plays muted. Never scroll-scrubbed.

**Why use it:**
- Social / creator / community apps that sell **belonging**, not dashboards
- Signature marquee + free-play culture film rebrand cleanly for any community product
- Client film is pure media (no burnt UI); storefront dual previews are separate agent burns
- Multi-type browse: **Hero** and **Landing Page**

Default lockup, marquee tokens, CTAs, and proof chips are a starting board only. Rewrite them until the page could only be your product. Keep ethics: no fake celebrity claims, no competitor brands in the marquee, no invented audited metrics presented as facts.

**How you build it:** download the product pack. Tell your AI: *Build VERVE SOCIAL using the files in this folder. Read PROMPT.md.*

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** Premium Gen-Z / creator social night. Canvas `#1A0A14`, cream `#FDF7FA`, hot rose `#EC4899`, amber `#F59E0B`. Syne (or Clash Display) 800 + Inter. **Not** Motionsites cyan kit, TikTok sticker kitsch, neon cyberpunk rain, or empty mesh-only wallpaper.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** High-end creator app launch night × bold display lockup × living culture film. Think funded social product marketing, not template mesh.

**Typography:**
- Display / H1: Syne or Clash Display 800, tracking `-0.04em`, line-height ~0.88, `clamp(2.75rem, 9vw, 6.75rem)`
- Rose second line: same family, `#EC4899`
- Body: Inter 400-500, 15-16px, cream ~68%
- Marquee: Inter/Syne 12-13px uppercase tracking ~0.22em
- Wordmark: Syne/Clash 13px bold tracking ~0.28em

**Color tokens:**

## Interaction law (non-negotiable)

1. Film free-plays muted loop. **Never** set `video.currentTime` from scroll.  
2. Framer stagger entrance on type/CTAs (moderate ease).  
3. Infinite marquee via CSS `translate3d` ~42s linear; edge mask fade; static under reduced motion.  
4. Desktop only: film wrap scale `1 → 1.06`, ScrollTrigger scrub `1.2`, start `top top` / end `bottom top`. Off below 768px.  
5. IntersectionObserver pause off-screen; cleanup on unmount (IO + ScrollTrigger kill).  
6. Reduced motion: poster still (`currentTime = 0` pause only), no parallax, static marquee row.
