## Promise (buyer-facing)

**ZERO ENERGY** is a full-page **3D range gallery**. The difference is the lineup. Six vessels share one dark stage. Visitors grab the can they want and turn the whole range by hand. Scroll then opens flavor, four proof beats, a hard closer, the pack, and FAQ. One piece does hero and landing page.

This is not a single hero can and not a photo slider. It is a private tasting of your range, then the brand story in the same breath.

**Why use it:**
- Energy drinks, sodas, coffee, water, wine, skincare, supplements, or any physical SKU that sells as a family
- Visitors choose with their hands, then read why it is theirs
- Six products, or fewer. The carousel is the range. The scroll is the argument.
- Every label, line, and color is meant to be replaced until a stranger knows it is yours

Default Zero Energy copy and six flavor labels are a starting board.

**How you build it:** download the product pack. Tell your AI:

> Build ZERO ENERGY using the files in this folder. Read START-HERE.md. This is my brand, not Zero Energy. Swap the six labels, the logo, and every line of copy. Keep the 3D range and the scroll journey.

Drop in your can art and your product names. Your AI rebuilds the gallery around your lineup.

## Layout Structure

- **Full-page machine.** One fixed viewport stage. No traditional long-page scrollbar as the product UX.
- **Pin-until-complete (mandatory):** the WebGL canvas and HUD stay pinned. Wheel, trackpad, and touch drive **virtual progress** (Lenis seeks the GSAP timeline). When this is the whole site, Lenis owns the document. When embedded in a longer page, **pin the stage until the closer chapter finishes, then release**. Never convert this to a tall sticky `N x 100vh` track.
- **Nav:** logo left. Links Gamme / Benefices / FAQ. Contact is an in-page jump to `#FAQ`. No outbound URLs.
- **Journey regions (in order):**
  1. 3D can carousel (grab + arrows + liquid pager)
  2. Flavor profile (title + body for the active can)
  3. Four benefit beats
  4. ZERO BULLSHIT mark
  5. Packshot
  6. Nine FAQ
  7. Closer
- **Desktop:** full HUD around the stage. Carousel spacing `3.5`. Camera FOV `20`.
- **Mobile:** HUD compresses. Touch drag on the canvas. Same chapter order. Targets ≥44px.

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** pin-until-complete 3D range + Lenis virtual progress. Not free-play film. Not `ScrollTrigger.scrub`.

| Event | Timing | Ease / notes |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Clock | Lenis `infinite: true`, `autoRaf: false` | Lenis scroll **seeks** the GSAP timeline. Do not switch to ST.scrub. |
| Virtual progress | 0 → 1 across the journey | Wheel / trackpad / touch. Stage stays fixed. |
| Embed release | progress 1 + scroll down | Unpin so the host page can continue. Progress 0 + scroll up also releases. |
| Can grab | pointer capture | Horizontal drag turns the range. Arrows and liquid pager stay in sync. |
| Carousel snap | debounce 250ms / 50ms | Snaps to nearest can. Spacing `3.5`. |
| Flavor HUD | on index change | SplitText lines mask, stagger `0.08s`, duration `0.7s`, ease `power3.out` |
| Benefits | chapter enter | SplitText line reveal, same stagger band `0.05-0.2s` |
| FAQ accordion | click / tap | Height + opacity, ~0.35s `power2.out` |
| Reduced motion | `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` | Settle on chapter 1. No grab thrash. No long timeline seek. Cans remain readable. |

**Do not:**
- Build a tall multi-vh sticky ScrollTrigger track
- Seek `video.currentTime` (there is no hero film)
- Auto-rotate the carousel as the signature (the hand turns the range)
- Animate layout width/height in a way that causes CLS

- Contrast: cream / white type on `#000`; flavor secondary used only as accent

## Performance Notes

- **Three 0.161.0 exact.** Raw module. No R3F. Do not bump to match other products.
- Isolate this Three from any host `three@0.185` / R3F (issuer alias or dedicated install).
- DPR: respect low-power / mobile caps already in `webgl-scene.js`. Do not raise them.
- Dispose WebGL renderer, composer, textures, and animation frame on unmount.
- Fonts: `font-display: swap` on local woff2.
- CSS sheets load as route-local links so they unmount with the page.
- JS budget: three + lenis + gsap + SplitText + one scene module. No extra 3D libraries.
- Poster / first paint: black stage, then cans. No layout shift from late HUD.
