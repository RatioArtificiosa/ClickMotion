**ELYSE** is a private wellness **pin-until-complete** scroll hero: one full-viewport earth stage, a 10-second golden-hour sanctuary film the playhead walks through four chapters from call to return. **PSAVE** (Perfect Scroll Video Engine): scroll aims, down plays forward, up plays reverse, the picture never jumps a frame. ## Motion Specification

**Mode:** Scroll-as-narrative · **pin-until-complete** · **PSAVE** (Perfect Scroll Video Engine). Scroll sets a destination on `3.6` viewports. Down plays the film forward at **1.2x**. Up plays it backward at the same **1.2x**, **exactly one 3-frame step per seek**. Never jump a frame. **When they lift, leftover dest keeps the film going a little** (friction, then a graceful stop, never a sudden screech). **Do not** autoplay-loop as primary. **Do not** seek `currentTime` across a jump.

**Hard ban:** tall multi-vh document scrollbar track (legacy `460vh` sticky pattern).  
**Hard ban:** seeking the playhead to a far destination (GSAP scrub, wheel-gain dump, Vertex `0.22`, old Revel `0.11` / GSAP lag `0.55`).  
**Hard ban:** a low-res frame buffer or canvas overlay for reverse (blurry, can repeat). Reverse walks the live video.  
**Hard ban:** releasing the pin because the destination hit 1 while the picture is still walking.  
**Hard ban:** a window wheel listener that keeps driving the film when the pointer is on the membership band.  
**Hard ban:** a replacement film with one I-frame (or long GOP + B-frames). Reverse will stall mid-film.  
**Hard ban:** pausing on the last wheel tick. Leftover dest is the lift. A tire-screech stop is a bug.

```
const VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS = 3.6; // GOLD: aim track ≡ old 460vh distance. Do not copy Revel 12.
const PSAVE_RATE = 1.2;        // film-seconds per wall-second, both directions
const PSAVE_FRAME = 1 / 24;    // never skip more than one frame in a forward tick
const PSAVE_REV_STRIDE = 3;    // reverse: exactly 3 frames per seek (0.125s)
const PSAVE_LIVE_MS = 220;     // still scrolling if last up/down is inside this window
```

**The lift (by design, not an accident):** scroll only aims. After they stop, leftover dest sits ahead of the playhead. Native `play()` at 1.2x keeps walking until dest arrives. That leftover is the “keeps going a little.” First **real** opposite gesture snaps dest onto the picture so reverse does not walk a stale forward aim. Ignore tiny opposite trackpad ticks (under ~32px inside the live window) so bounce does not kill leftover dest. If a replacement film is long or eventful (slow then a kick) and leftover dest dies, add Revel’s floor: dest at least `0.55s` of film ahead after last intent, ease `playbackRate` from 1.2 toward ~0.42 over that last half-second. Elyse’s 10s even sanctuary film usually keeps leftover dest without the extra floor. Family spec: `docs/PSAVE.md` §9.1 and §5B.

**Earn vs perceived time:** Elyse’s film is even sanctuary time. The 3.6 track is locked. Do not shorten it. Do not match another SKU’s viewports. Vertex (also PSAVE, even asteroid film) stays at **3.6** plus the 0.55s dest floor. Revel (also PSAVE) uses **12** viewports because that 20s film is designed slow-then-kick; halfway (she leaves the phone) must take ~5–6 scrolls. If you swap Elyse’s video, size the new track to the **story beat**, then re-encode GOP 3. Do not copy 3.6 or 12 blindly.

**Rebuild algorithm (mandatory):** one `100dvh` stage in **normal document flow**. `#request` is the **next sibling**. Do not `overflow: hidden` the page. Do not import ScrollTrigger, do not use `position: sticky`, and do not build a `460vh` spacer. Gestures map **1:1** onto `3.6` viewports and only move the **destination**. First real opposite gesture snaps destination to the picture. Ignore tiny opposite ticks so leftover dest survives the lift. Each frame, walk the picture toward that destination at **1.2x**, never more than **1/24s** of film on a forward fallback tick. **Down:** muted `play()` at `playbackRate 1.2`. After they lift, leftover dest keeps play going a little, then it arrives and pauses (friction stop). **Up:** first real up-scroll cancels a pending forward destination. Walk `currentTime` backward exactly one 3-frame step per seek, **during the gesture and after**. Wait `seeked`. Never seek to the stop point. No loop. No low-res buffer. Copy, chapters, and the gold bar follow the **playhead**. Release only when the **picture** is at `0+up` / `1+down`. Test live `currentTime` as well as the playhead ref (settle can stop ~1 frame short). After release the **page** owns scroll until the stage docks at the top. Pointer on `#request` never drives the film. **Opening frame:** do not set `elyse-nature-v1.webp` as the HTML `<video poster>` (it is a mid-film look-down). Kick-seek `0.04 → 0`, wait `seeked`, fade the film in. First scroll continues from heads-up. Reduced motion may use that poster as a stage fallback and seek to `~0.42`.

| Element | Motion |
|---------|--------|
| Film | PSAVE: play toward destination forward or reverse, never jump a frame |
| Stage | Pinned 100dvh; no tall page track |
| Wheel | 1:1 onto the 3.6 aim track (destination only) |
| Lift | Leftover dest: picture keeps going a little, then friction-stops. Never screech. |
| Chapters | Crossfade eyebrow/title/body from **playhead** (0.7-0.9s rise/fade) |
| Progress bar | `scaleX(playhead)` gold gradient |
| CTA hover | translateY -1px on primary |

```
ffmpeg -y -i your-film.mp4 -an -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -preset slow -crf 16 -g 3 -keyint_min 3 -bf 0 -sc_threshold 0 -movflags +faststart your-film-psave.mp4
```
