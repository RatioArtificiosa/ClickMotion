## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **ELYSE** - private luxury wellness retreat house. One sticky full-viewport stage over a golden-hour sanctuary film (dual tree-of-life faces meeting over a valley river). **Scroll owns the film timeline** (scrub). Dark earth ink, cream type, soft gold. Aman / Six Senses restraint. Invitation only. Not cream spa SaaS. Not climate-tech green. Not fashion pearl Revel.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** Six Senses / Aman digital quiet × private-bank type density × editorial scroll chapters. One film. Four chapters. Scroll owns time. Stop.

**Typography:**
- Brand / wordmark: Cormorant Garamond 1rem, tracking `0.34em`, uppercase
- Brand line: Inter 10px, tracking `0.22em`, soft cream gold
- Eyebrow: Inter 11px, tracking `0.26em`, gold `#C9A46A`
- H1 display: Cormorant Garamond, `clamp(2.4rem, 6.2vw, 5.35rem)`, line-height 1.02, tracking `-0.025em`, two stacked lines
- Body: Inter 300, ~15-17px, line-height 1.7, cream 78%
- Chapter indices: Inter 10px, tracking `0.22em`
- CTA: Inter 12px, tracking `0.16em`, uppercase

**Color tokens:**
| Token | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Stage ink | `#0B0907` |
| Cream | `#F4EDE3` / `#F7F1E8` |
| Gold | `#C9A46A` |
| Warm highlight | `#F0D9A8` |
| Veil | deep earth edges; keep center faces + sun luminous |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** purple mesh, neon pills, emoji, rainbow type, Motionsites pill docks, free-play autoplay as primary mode (scroll must own film), mid-frame multi-paragraph sales essays, dense SaaS metric grids on the film face, cream spa Aether clone, climate Terra green tech chrome.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Elyse must not become |
|----|------------------------|
| Aether | Cream ocean "Breathe. Be." wellness app |
| Terra | Climate-tech forest / renewable energy |
| Meridian | Dark coastal private residences |
| Revel | Light pearl fashion phone-break |
| Sable | Sparse holiday fashion free-play walk |

## Layout Structure

- **Hero:** sticky full-viewport stage inside tall scroll track (~460vh)
- **Film:** full bleed `object-fit: cover`, `object-position: center 48%`, scale ~1.02
- **Nav:** absolute top - brand + Private Wellness Retreats, center links, Request invitation
- **Progress:** 1px gold gradient under nav, driven by scroll progress (not video free-play clock alone)
- **Copy:** lower-left chapter eyebrow + two-line H1 + body; right chapter index rail 01-04
- **Signature:** optional soft gold filament at ~46% height (meeting of the faces)
- **Finale CTAs:** Begin a private inquiry + View the calendar
- **Closing band:** Membership - wealth in stillness + stats + Request an introduction
- **Safe insets:** horizontal `clamp(1.35rem, 4.5vw, 4rem)`; type never flush to edge

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** Scroll-as-narrative. Map scroll progress (0-1) to `video.currentTime` (0-duration) with GSAP ScrollTrigger `scrub: ~0.55`. Pin sticky 100vh stage. **Do not** autoplay-loop as primary.

| Element | Motion |
|---------|--------|
| Film | Scrubbed by scroll; pause video; seek on progress |
| Stage | Sticky pin for ~460vh track |
| Chapters | Crossfade eyebrow/title/body on active chapter (0.7-0.9s rise/fade) |
| Progress bar | `scaleX(scrollProgress)` gold gradient |
| CTA hover | translateY -1px on primary |
| Reduced motion | 100vh static track, mid-film still (~0.42), no scrub required |
