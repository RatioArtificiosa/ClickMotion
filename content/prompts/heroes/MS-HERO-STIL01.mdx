## Promise (buyer-facing)

**STILL** is a full-viewport **mindfulness pin-until-complete** scroll hero: one night-sky stage, a 30-second cosmic transformation film the playhead walks through five soft chapters from arid quiet to living cosmos. **Dual process = PSAVE + No Scroller.** **PSAVE** (Perfect Scroll Video Engine): scroll aims, down plays forward, up plays reverse, the picture never jumps a frame. **No Scroller:** the page does not physically scroll during the journey. Not a tall 960vh sticky track. Not hybrid Option A idle free-play. Not Meridian estate scrub. Not Elyse sanctuary gold.

**Why use it:**
- Mindfulness, meditation, sleep, and wellness brands that sell **calm as craft**
- Dual-process signature: scroll aims a long even cosmos film; the picture plays; the page stays still
- Cormorant + Inter restages cleanly for any soft-premium wellness brand
- Client HD is pure film (no burnt UI). Storefront dual previews are separate

Default copy (**Soften. / Begin again.** through **Come home / to yourself.**) is a starting board. Restage chapters, whispers, CTAs, and stats until it is unmistakably yours.

**How you build it:** download the product pack (prompt + source + film). Tell your AI: *Build STILL using the files in this folder. Read PROMPT.md and follow it. Prefer source/StillMindfulnessHero.tsx. Dual process: PSAVE plus No Scroller. Pin-until-complete. Scroll aims on 12 viewports (30s even cosmos: two flicks must not dump the film). Down-scroll plays the film forward at 1.2x. Up-scroll plays it backward at the same 1.2x, one 3-frame step per seek. Never jump a frame. When they lift, leftover dest plus a 0.55s dest floor keeps the film going a little, then rate eases to a stop. Copy and the mint bar follow the picture. Release only when the picture arrives. After release the host page may continue. Replacement films must be re-encoded GOP 3, no B-frames. Do not seek the playhead across the film. Do not restore hybrid Option A, a 5s idle free-play, GSAP ScrollTrigger, or a 960vh sticky track. Do not install gsap.*

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** Scroll-as-narrative · **pin-until-complete** (**No Scroller**) · **PSAVE** (Perfect Scroll Video Engine). **Dual process.** Scroll sets a destination on `12` viewports. Down plays the film forward at **1.2x**. Up plays it backward at the same **1.2x**, **exactly one 3-frame step per seek**. Never jump a frame. **When they lift, leftover dest plus a 0.55s dest floor keeps the film going a little**, then rate eases to a stop. Copy, whispers, chapter markers, and the mint bar follow the **picture**, not the wheel target. Release only when the **picture** arrives at `0` (up) or `1` (down). **Do not** autoplay-loop as primary. **Do not** seek `currentTime` across a jump. **Do not** restore 5s idle free-play.

**Hard ban:** tall multi-vh document scrollbar track (legacy `960vh` sticky pattern).
**Hard ban:** hybrid Option A (idle >= 5000ms free-play with frozen scroll and one reclaim `scrollTo`).
**Hard ban:** seeking the playhead to a far destination (GSAP `ScrollTrigger` scrub `0.45`, Meridian gain `0.22`, `video.currentTime = dest * duration` on a large delta).
**Hard ban:** a 3.6 aim track on this 30s film (two flicks dump dest; feels like watching a video). Locked earn is **12**.
**Hard ban:** `WHEEL_GAIN`, swipe cap, GSAP lag, `STILL_IDLE_MS`, `TRACK_VH`, mode chip.
**Hard ban:** a low-res frame buffer or canvas overlay for reverse. Reverse walks the live video.
**Hard ban:** releasing the pin because the destination hit 1 while the picture is still walking.
**Hard ban:** a window wheel listener that keeps driving the film after the pin has released.
**Hard ban:** a replacement film with one I-frame (or long GOP + B-frames). Reverse will stall mid-film.
**Hard ban:** pausing on the last wheel tick. The stop is friction, then a graceful slowdown.
**Hard ban:** installing `gsap` or importing `ScrollTrigger`.

```
const VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS = 12;  // GOLD: 30s even cosmos. Not Elyse/Vertex 3.6. Not old 960vh.
const PSAVE_RATE = 1.2;        // film-seconds per wall-second, both directions
const PSAVE_FRAME = 1 / 24;    // never skip more than one frame in a forward tick
const PSAVE_REV_STRIDE = 3;    // reverse: exactly 3 frames per seek (0.125s)
const PSAVE_LIVE_MS = 280;     // still scrolling if last up/down is inside this window
const PSAVE_COAST_SEC = 0.55;  // leftover dest floor after last real intent
const PSAVE_EASE_SEC = 0.55;   // forward rate tapers over last leftover film-seconds
const PSAVE_FLIP_DEADZONE_PX = 32; // ignore tiny opposite trackpad ticks
```

**Earn vs perceived time:** this film is **even** cosmos time (desert greening the whole 30s, not a late kick). Even films that are **short** (Elyse 10s, Vertex 12s) lock at **3.6**. This film is **long**. Two 1800px flicks on 3.6 dump dest past the last chapter. On **12**, those same flicks aim about one third of the film (~10s of picture). The visitor earns the lush valley. Do not copy Elyse 3.6. Do not restore 960vh. Do not flatten the edit.

**The lift:** leftover dest is the Elyse stop. On this 30s film dest still dies (1.2x eats it; a tiny opposite tick snaps dest). After last real intent, dest must sit at least `0.55s` of film ahead. Ignore opposite ticks under `32px`. Forward `playbackRate` stays 1.2 until leftover dest is under `0.55s`, then tapers toward ~0.42. Friction, then a graceful stop. This `0.55` is **not** old GSAP `SCRUB 0.45`. Live window is **280ms** (Revel-class, not Vertex 220).

**Rebuild algorithm (mandatory):** one `100dvh` stage in **normal document flow**. There is **no** required next-sibling band inside this product. Do not `overflow: hidden` the page (a host embed must be able to scroll after release). Do not import ScrollTrigger or GSAP. Do not use `position: sticky`. Do not build a `960vh` spacer. Gestures map **1:1** onto `12` viewports and only move the **destination**. First **real** opposite gesture snaps destination to the picture. Ignore tiny opposite ticks. Each frame, walk the picture toward that destination at **1.2x**, never more than **1/24s** of film on a forward fallback tick. **Down:** muted `play()` at `playbackRate 1.2`, ease over the last `0.55s` of leftover dest. After they lift, apply the `0.55s` dest floor once if dest is closer than that. **Up:** first real up-scroll cancels a pending forward destination. Walk `currentTime` backward exactly one 3-frame step per seek, **during the gesture and after**. Wait `seeked`. Never seek to the stop point. No loop. No low-res buffer. Copy, chapters, whispers, and the mint bar follow the **playhead**. Release only when the **picture** is at `0+up` / `1+down`. Test live `currentTime` as well as the playhead ref (settle can stop ~1 frame short). After release the **page** owns scroll until the stage docks at the top. **Opening frame:** kick-seek `0.04 → 0`, wait `seeked`, fade the film in (`.is-ready`). Reduced motion keeps the poster and chapter 1 only.

| Element | Motion |
|---------|--------|
| Film | PSAVE: play toward destination forward or reverse, never jump a frame |
| Stage | Pinned 100dvh; no tall page track |
| Wheel | 1:1 onto the 12 aim track (destination only) |
| Lift | 0.55s dest floor + rate ease. Friction, then a graceful stop. |
| Chapters | Crossfade ~0.75s cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1) from **playhead** |
| Progress bar | `scaleX(playhead)` mint → soft violet |
| Whispers | Soft mint when that chapter is active |
| Reduced motion | 100dvh static stage, poster + chapter 1, no PSAVE |

Capture helper: `window.__msScrollNarrative = { setProgress, getProgress, getTarget, productId: "MS-HERO-STIL01" }`. `getProgress` is the picture. `getTarget` is leftover dest. Root marker: `data-still-drive="psave"`.
