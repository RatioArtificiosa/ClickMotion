## Promise (buyer-facing)

**PRISM** is a full-viewport **creative identity pin-until-complete** scroll hero: one studio-mist stage, a 48-second multi-face sculpture film the playhead walks through three acts (Atelier, Proof, Invite) while liquid-glass panels of many sizes float on **both left and right**. **Dual process = PSAVE + No Scroller.** **PSAVE** (Perfect Scroll Video Engine): scroll aims, down plays forward, up plays reverse, the picture never jumps a frame. **No Scroller:** the page does not physically scroll during the journey. Not a tall 520vh sticky track. Not GSAP ScrollTrigger seek-scrub. Not Meridian estate scrub. Not Revel pearl fashion. Not a left-column-only essay.

**Why use it:**
- Creative studios, identity houses, and fashion-adjacent brands that sell **many faces as one system**
- Dual-process signature: scroll aims a long even faces film; the picture plays; glass panels follow what is on screen; the page stays still
- Syne + DM Sans restages cleanly for any prestige identity studio
- Client HD is pure sculpture film (no burnt UI). Storefront dual previews are separate

Default copy (Now booking through Book a studio intro) is a starting board. Restage panel titles, metrics, quotes, and the atelier band until it is unmistakably yours. **Never collapse to a single left H1 column.**

**How you build it:** download the product pack (prompt + source + film). Tell your AI: *Build PRISM using the files in this folder. Read PROMPT.md and follow it. Prefer source/PrismLiquidGlass.tsx. Dual process: PSAVE plus No Scroller. Pin-until-complete. Scroll aims on 12 viewports (47.63s even faces: two flicks must not dump the film). Down-scroll plays the film forward at 1.2x. Up-scroll plays it backward at the same 1.2x, one 3-frame step per seek. Never jump a frame. When they lift, leftover dest plus a 0.55s dest floor keeps the film going a little, then rate eases to a stop. Glass panels, moment pill, and the violet bar follow the picture. Release only when the picture arrives. After release the atelier band may scroll in. Replacement films must be re-encoded GOP 3, no B-frames. Do not seek the playhead across the film. Do not restore GSAP ScrollTrigger, a 520vh sticky track, or left-column-only layout. Do not install gsap.*

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** Scroll-as-narrative · **pin-until-complete** (**No Scroller**) · **PSAVE** (Perfect Scroll Video Engine). **Dual process.** Scroll sets a destination on `12` viewports. Down plays the film forward at **1.2x**. Up plays it backward at the same **1.2x**, **exactly one 3-frame step per seek**. Never jump a frame. **When they lift, leftover dest plus a 0.55s dest floor keeps the film going a little**, then rate eases to a stop. Glass panels, moment pill, and the violet bar follow the **picture**, not the wheel target. Release only when the **picture** arrives at `0` (up) or `1` (down). Then `#atelier` may scroll in. **Do not** autoplay-loop as primary. **Do not** seek `currentTime` across a jump.

**Hard ban:** tall multi-vh document scrollbar track (legacy `520vh` sticky pattern).
**Hard ban:** GSAP `ScrollTrigger` scrub `0.55` mapping progress onto `currentTime`.
**Hard ban:** seeking the playhead to a far destination (`video.currentTime = dest * duration` on a large delta).
**Hard ban:** a 3.6 aim track on this 47.63s film (two flicks dump dest; feels like watching a video). Locked earn is **12**.
**Hard ban:** `WHEEL_GAIN`, swipe cap, GSAP lag.
**Hard ban:** a low-res frame buffer or canvas overlay for reverse. Reverse walks the live video.
**Hard ban:** releasing the pin because the destination hit 1 while the picture is still walking.
**Hard ban:** a window wheel listener that keeps driving the film after the pin has released.
**Hard ban:** a replacement film with one I-frame (or long GOP + B-frames). Reverse will stall mid-film.
**Hard ban:** pausing on the last wheel tick. The stop is friction, then a graceful slowdown.
**Hard ban:** installing `gsap` or importing `ScrollTrigger`.
**Hard ban:** left-column-only layout.

```
const VIRTUAL_VIEWPORTS = 12;  // GOLD: 47.63s even faces. Not Elyse/Vertex 3.6. Not old 520vh.
const PSAVE_RATE = 1.2;        // film-seconds per wall-second, both directions
const PSAVE_FRAME = 1 / 24;    // never skip more than one frame in a forward tick
const PSAVE_REV_STRIDE = 3;    // reverse: exactly 3 frames per seek (0.125s)
const PSAVE_LIVE_MS = 280;     // still scrolling if last up/down is inside this window
const PSAVE_COAST_SEC = 0.55;  // leftover dest floor after last real intent
const PSAVE_EASE_SEC = 0.55;   // forward rate tapers over last leftover film-seconds
const PSAVE_FLIP_DEADZONE_PX = 32; // ignore tiny opposite trackpad ticks
```

**Earn vs perceived time:** this film is **even** faces time (the sculpture morphs the whole 47.63s, not a late kick). Even films that are **short** (Elyse 10s, Vertex 12s) lock at **3.6**. This film is **long**. Two 1800px flicks on 3.6 dump dest past Invite. On **12**, those same flicks aim about one third of the film. The visitor earns every face. Same number as Still and Revel, **different reason** (length, not a kick). Do not copy Elyse 3.6. Do not restore 520vh. Do not flatten the edit.

**The lift:** leftover dest is the Elyse stop. On this 48s film dest still dies (1.2x eats it; a tiny opposite tick snaps dest). After last real intent, dest must sit at least `0.55s` of film ahead. Ignore opposite ticks under `32px`. Forward `playbackRate` stays 1.2 until leftover dest is under `0.55s`, then tapers toward ~0.42. Friction, then a graceful stop. This `0.55` is **not** old GSAP `scrub 0.55`. Live window is **280ms** (Revel/Still-class, not Vertex 220).

**Rebuild algorithm (mandatory):** one `100dvh` stage in **normal document flow**. Next sibling is `#atelier`. Do not `overflow: hidden` the page. Do not import ScrollTrigger or GSAP. Do not use `position: sticky`. Do not build a `520vh` spacer. Gestures map **1:1** onto `12` viewports and only move the **destination**. First **real** opposite gesture snaps destination to the picture. Ignore tiny opposite ticks. Each frame, walk the picture toward that destination at **1.2x**, never more than **1/24s** of film on a forward fallback tick. **Down:** muted `play()` at `playbackRate 1.2`, ease over the last `0.55s` of leftover dest. After they lift, apply the `0.55s` dest floor once if dest is closer than that. **Up:** first real up-scroll cancels a pending forward destination. Walk `currentTime` backward exactly one 3-frame step per seek, **during the gesture and after**. Wait `seeked`. Never seek to the stop point. No loop. No low-res buffer. Glass panels, moment pill, and the violet bar follow the **playhead**. Release only when the **picture** is at `0+up` / `1+down`. Test live `currentTime` as well as the playhead ref (settle can stop ~1 frame short). After release the **page** owns scroll until the stage docks at the top. **Opening frame:** kick-seek `0.04 → 0`, wait `seeked`. Reduced motion keeps a **mid composition at 0.42** (readable Proof-adjacent panels). No chase.

| Element | Motion |
|---------|--------|
| Film | PSAVE: play toward destination forward or reverse, never jump a frame |
| Stage | Pinned 100dvh; no tall page track |
| Wheel | 1:1 onto the 12 aim track (destination only) |
| Lift | 0.55s dest floor + rate ease. Friction, then a graceful stop. |
| Panels | Opacity + 36px side enter from **playhead** (fade 0.045) |
| Moment pill | Atelier / Proof / Invite from playhead |
| Progress bar | `scaleX(playhead)` violet → fuchsia → cyan |
| Reduced motion | 100dvh static stage, playhead 0.42, no PSAVE |

Capture helper: `window.__msScrollNarrative = { setProgress, getProgress, getTarget, productId: "MS-HERO-PRSM01" }`. `getProgress` is the picture. `getTarget` is leftover dest. Root marker: `data-prism-drive="psave"`.
