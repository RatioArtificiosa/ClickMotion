## Promise (buyer-facing)

**Acne Secret** is a full-viewport **private clear-skin HVCO hero**: near-black prestige, gold signal, combat-cadence WARNING energy, and a timed film stage that holds centered at half width over dark frosted blur, then docks left so the lead form owns the right. The brand name stays locked until email. Demo unlock may use a labeled synthetic name until you restage your real brand.

**Why use it:**
- Skincare and clear-skin brands that sell **access and curiosity** before the hard pitch
- Sabri Suby-class structure: sell the click / opt-in, not the whole catalog on first paint
- Framer layout phases + free-play film restage cleanly for any product film
- Client film is pure media (no burnt UI); storefront dual previews are separate presentation burns

Default copy is a starting board. Restage headline, HVCO name, bullets, CTA, and unlock brand until it is unmistakably yours. Keep ethics: no medical cure claims, no body shame, no fake clinical stats.

**How you build it:** download the product pack (prompt + source + film). Tell your AI: *Build Acne Secret using the files in this folder. Read PROMPT.md.*

## Interaction law (non-negotiable)

1. Film **free-plays** muted (`autoPlay` `muted` `loop` `playsInline`). **Never** set `video.currentTime` from scroll.  
2. Cinema hold `CINEMA_S` default **15s** (wall clock and/or video clock; dock when max reaches `CINEMA_S`).  
3. After cinema: moderate dock ease (~**1.05s**, cubic-bezier `0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1`); form fades/slides in (~0.85s, slight delay).  
4. Brand name **locked** until a valid email submit; then reveal unlock card.  
5. `prefers-reduced-motion`: skip cinema hold; show docked layout immediately; film may pause on poster.  
6. IntersectionObserver: pause film when off-screen; cleanup on unmount.  
7. Optional QA: `window` event `ms-acne-force-dock` forces dock; optional `__MS_CAPTURE_CLOCK` presentation scrub for burns (ignore in normal buyer builds).

## Motion Specification

**Mode:** Free-play film + timed layout phase (cinema → dock). **Not** scroll-scrub. **Not** wallpaper-only without HVCO dock.

| Layer | Spec |
|-------|------|
| Film | muted loop autoplay playsInline; pause off-screen |
| Cinema | centered ~50vw, dark frosted blur plate, optional progress hairline |
| Dock | film left ~44% / form right; duration ~1.05s; ease `cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1)` |
| Form entrance | opacity + x slide ~0.85s, delay ~0.25s, soft ease |
| Reduced motion | docked immediately; no cinema hold required |
| Narrow | short beat or stack; no forced 15s hold for usability |
