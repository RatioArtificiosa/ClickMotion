# Brand craft research — STILL vs Calm / Headspace

**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Purpose:** Inform copy, color, and overlay density for MS-HERO-STIL01 without cloning trademarks.

## Calm (calm.com)

- Hero line class: **Calm your mind. Change your life.**
- Promise buckets: stress less, sleep better, live happier
- Delivery: soft, sparse, premium audio-first; lavender/night calm blues
- Strength: emotional permission + sleep brand equity
- Gap we beat: rarely owns a **cinematic pin narrative**; mostly static marketing + app UI

## Headspace (headspace.com)

- Positioning: lifelong guide to better mental health; evidence-based tools
- Promise: more joy, less stress, better sleep; 1000+ guided exercises
- Delivery: friendly structured education + orange/brand illustration heritage; productized courses
- Strength: habit framing, science credibility
- Gap we beat: site motion is lighter; we own **film time as practice metaphor**

## STILL differentiators (locked)

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Name | STILL (ownable, not a clone) |
| Visual | Cosmic growth film (arid → verdant → sky) |
| Motion | Dual process: PSAVE + No Scroller. Scroll aims. Film plays. Lift coasts. (2026-08-12 idle hybrid is banned.) |
| Type | Cormorant / soft serif + Inter |
| Accent | Mint `#8fd0c8` on night `#070b12` (Calm softness × cosmos film) |

## Overlay system (beyond their sites)

1. Five chapter lockups with progress ranges  
2. Whisper words (Breathe in / Unclench / Ease / Expand / Return)  
3. No center breath ring (film stays clean; old auto-mode ring is banned)  
4. Mint→violet progress hairline  
5. Chapter index 01–05  
6. End CTAs + three quiet stats  

## Forbidden

- Trademark names Headspace / Calm in buyer UI  
- Medical cure claims  
- Emoji, purple SaaS mesh, neon  
- Em dashes in customer copy
