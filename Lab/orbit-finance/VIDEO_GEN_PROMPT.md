1. Prefer **architecture + light first**. If the model melts faces, drop people entirely.  
2. If any **readable numbers or logos** appear, discard the take.  
3. Gold should feel **jewelry / warm metal**, not yellow highlighter.  
4. Avoid “generic city drone spam” - seek **private, expensive, specific** interiors or refined dusk glass.  
5. Export highest quality silent master; we encode client HD + poster in-repo.  
6. If you get two strong 12s clips of the **same grade**, we can soft-crossfade in ffmpeg later - still no UI.  
7. A physical **gold ring sculpture** beat is excellent for brand metaphor without burning a logo.
