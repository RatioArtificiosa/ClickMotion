# ORBIT FINANCE — brand craft (Forbes / private-bank bar)

## Positioning

**Quiet wealth. Global control. No shouting.**

ORBIT is the neobank that feels like a modern private bank: multi-currency, calm security, elevated taste. The hero sells **trust and elevation** before features.

## Signature system

1. **Film** — vault/architecture wealth cinema (pure media)  
2. **Orbital ring** — gold geometric path UI (brand mark of “Orbit”)  
3. **Serif lockup** — DM Serif “Money, elevated.”  
4. **Trust proof** — calm metrics (honest placeholders)  
5. **Free-play only** — never scroll-scrub the film  

## Color discipline

- Vault navy `#0B1426`  
- Cream `#F7F4EC`  
- Orbit gold `#C9A84C`  
- **No cyan. No hot pink. No neon green crypto.**

## Type

- Display: **DM Serif Display**  
- UI/body: **Inter**  

## Voice

- Elevated, short, confident  
- No em dashes  
- No “disrupt banking forever” hype  
- No guaranteed yield claims  

## Film voids for type

Keep **left third** and **lower third** darker / softer so cream type and CTAs stay legible.
