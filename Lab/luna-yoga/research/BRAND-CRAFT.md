# Brand craft - BLOOM (kids & teen girls yoga)

**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Purpose:** Ultra-premium craft direction for an online yoga course + mobile app aimed at kids and teen girls. No trademark clones.

---

## Market poles (learn craft, do not copy names into UI)

### Calm / Headspace class (adult wellness)

- Soft permission language, sparse type, sleep + stress buckets  
- Gap for us: adult, muted, audio-first - **not** girl-class joy or dual-age courses

### Peloton / Alo / Alo Moves class (premium fitness)

- Strong commerce, class cards, instructor heat  
- Gap for us: often adult athletic chrome; we need **softer belonging** without looking like a gym

### Kids wellness apps (generic market)

- Often: loud primary colors, emoji, cartoon mascots, “FUN!!!” all-caps  
- Gap we beat: **Forbes-parent × cool-teen** register - elevated, never babyish, never cringe

### What BLOOM owns

| Axis | Choice |
|------|--------|
| Emotional job | “I want to join the circle and feel calm + cool” |
| Audience | Kids ~7-12 and teens ~13-17 (toggle on hero) |
| Film world | Bright multi-girl stylized class (operator film) |
| Commerce | Online interactive course + cell app download |
| Tone | Soft power, friendship, ritual, confidence - not diet talk, not medical claims |

## Visual system (draft tokens)

| Token | Value | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| Canvas | `#fff8f5` warm paper or soft lilac wash | Matches sunlit studio film |
| Ink | `#2a2438` soft night plum | Readable, not harsh black |
| Lilac | `#c4a8e8` | Matches costume energy in film |
| Peach | `#ffb5a7` | Warm CTA heat without neon |
| Butter | `#ffe8a3` | Kids path accent (sparing) |
| Mint soft | `#b8e0d2` | Teens path calm accent (sparing) |
| Display | Plus Jakarta / Nunito / rounded geometric **or** soft serif for brand word only | Premium app, not comic book |
| Body | Inter / Plus Jakarta 400-600 | Clean UI for course modules |

**Material:** cream glass, soft shadows, full pills, 12-16px radius cards.  
**Anti-slop ban:** purple SaaS mesh, neon cyan, emoji spam, all-caps scream headlines, stock white spa with empty yoga mat, dark cyber wellness.

## Copy craft (examples - not final)

**Kids path**

- Badge: Yoga for growing girls  
- H1: Soft strength. Big smiles.  
- Body: Short classes you can finish. Breathe, stretch, and feel proud in your own body.  
- CTA: Start free class · Get the app  

**Teens path**

- Badge: Interactive course + app  
- H1: Your calm. Your circle.  
- Body: Flows for busy school days, soft nights, and real confidence - no judgment, just show up.  
- CTA: Join free · Download app  

**Module chips (draft)**

Kids: Breathe · Stretch · Animal flows · Wind-down  
Teens: Focus · Flow · Strength soft · Sleep wind-down  

**Forbidden copy**

- Weight loss, body shame, “fix the summer”  
- Medical cure claims  
- Trademark: Headspace, Calm, Alo, Peloton in UI  
- Em dashes in customer-facing strings

## Motion craft

- Free-play film (class is alive)  
- Entrance under 1.2s total stagger  
- Hover on modules &lt;100ms opacity/translate  
- Age toggle: 200-300ms crossfade of copy stack only (film stays)  
- Reduced motion: poster still, no stagger
