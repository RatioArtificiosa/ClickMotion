## Why this product exists

An **online interactive yoga course + mobile app** for **kids and teen girls** who want calm that still feels cool: soft power, friendship energy, real poses, no lecture vibes.

The operator film is a bright 3D stylized class of diverse girls flowing together in a sunlit studio. That is the emotional product: **you belong in the circle**.
