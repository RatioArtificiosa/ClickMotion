## Promise (buyer-facing)

TILT is a mid-page **close** that reads as a fashion-house recovery of Y2K, not a HitCounter. One headline. One street. One chrome bead that takes a single lap around the button, then parks.

**Why use it:**
- The bead is the design. The board never says orbit
- Stage is bubblegum. Chrome is the metal
- Every line is a slot
- Sits after Verve, Actually!, or Dopamine without copying their film

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **TILT** - mid-page **waitlist / CTA**. Stage `#F7C6D8`. Ink `#1A1A1A`. Metal `#E8E8EC`. Unbounded display. Syne UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** arcade as atelier. Radius 12px. Not clipart 2000s.

**Design language:** `arcade-atelier` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `chrome-bubblegum`  
**Motion primitive:** `orbit-trinket` (12px path, 2400ms, one lap)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `pop-in`. Land ~880ms. Orbit after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Unbounded 800
- Headline: Unbounded 800, `clamp(2.4rem, 7.6vw, 5.2rem)`
- Period: Syne ~17px
- Button: Syne 700 uppercase

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** orbit-trinket. After land, one 10px chrome bead travels a 12px radius once in 2400ms, then parks. Confirm restages one lap.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `pop-in`.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS keyframes, one cycle.

**Reduced motion:** parked bead. One click confirms.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used.
