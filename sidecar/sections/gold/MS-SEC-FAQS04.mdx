## Promise (buyer-facing)

SLIP is a mid-page **FAQ** that reads as a tile shop, not a help center. One question. The answer plate morphs from sharp to wet.

**Why use it:**
- The morph is the design. The board never says mercury
- Stage is celadon. Wet white is the catch
- Sits after Elyse, STILL, or Aether without copying Kiln, Helm, or Ember

## Design System

Stage `#C5D5C8`. Ink `#1C2E28`. Accent `#F4FBF6`. Fraunces display. Outfit UI.

**Primitive:** `mercury-morph` (radius 4 to 28, scale 1.03, 640ms). **Enter:** `typeset-rise`.

**Anti-slop:** accordion kit, mint SaaS, glaze-drip clone.
