## Promise (buyer-facing)

REEL is a mid-page **testimonials** board that reads as a darkroom, not a quote carousel. The quote comes up like a print.

**Why use it:**
- The develop is the design. The board never says polaroid
- Stage is oxblood. Cream is the catch
- Sits after Elyse, STILL, or Aether without copying Ember, Kiln, or Port

## Design System

Stage `#5C1218`. Ink `#F6E6D8`. Fraunces italic display. Outfit UI.

**Primitive:** `polaroid-develop` (1400ms, contrast 0.35, brightness 1.4). **Enter:** `blackout-lift`.

**Anti-slop:** avatar cards, 5-star rows, logo marquee.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Masthead, quote, street.
