## Promise (buyer-facing)

TALLY is a mid-page **stats section** that reads as a desk blotter, not four tiles. One figure holds the page and counts. Visitors do not scan a kit of bouncing numbers. They sit with a path, an uptime, a notional, or a floor.

**Why use it:**
- Your proof feels written, not animated for show
- One figure at a time. The tape holds the rest
- Every venue, clock, and figure is a slot for your book
- Sits after Axiom, Orbit, or Nexus without copying their film
- Built for desks that measure before they announce

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **TALLY** - mid-page **proof / stats** (not a hero, not four cards). Stage white `#FFFFFF`. Ink `#0A2540`. One accent `#635BFF` as the count hairline only. Never a fill. Inter display. IBM Plex Mono labels.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** Stripe dashboard restraint × a clearing blotter × Axiom density. Quiet chrome. Tables welcome. No purple glass.

**Design language:** `calm-fintech`  
**Token theme:** `stripe-trust-light`  
**Motion primitive:** `count-on-scroll` (900ms, ease-out cubic, once per restage)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `settle-trade`. High damping. Land ~820ms. Interaction after land. Reduced motion skips enter.

**Typography:**
- Figure: Inter 500, `clamp(3.25rem, 11vw, 7.5rem)`, tabular-nums, tracking `-0.055em`
- Unit: IBM Plex Mono, 12–16px, uppercase
- Name: Inter 500, `clamp(1.15rem, 2.2vw, 1.5rem)`
- Labels / masthead / tape / CTA: IBM Plex Mono, 9–11px, uppercase tracking `0.14–0.2em`

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** count-on-scroll. After land, the active figure counts 0 → target in 900ms, ease-out cubic. The accent hairline `scaleX` follows. Retriggers when the tape changes. Not a forever ticker.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `settle-trade`. Type settles 14px. CSS cubic standing in for the high-damping spring. Interaction (tape, CTA, arrows) starts after land.

**Engine:** CSS enter. RequestAnimationFrame count.

**Reduced motion:** final number immediately. Hairline full. No settle.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used. This is not a scroll-narrative section. Count is once on view / restage, not a tall scrub.
