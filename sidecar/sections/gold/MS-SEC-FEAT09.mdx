## Promise (buyer-facing)

PIKE is a mid-page **features** board that reads as a boardwalk room, not a bento. One machine name. It folds 60 degrees and lands.

**Why use it:**
- The fold is the design. The board never says kaleidoscope
- Stage is bubblegum. Chrome is the catch
- Sits after Verve, Actually!, or Dopamine without copying Tilt or Brine

## Design System

Stage `#F7C6D8`. Ink `#1A1A1A`. Unbounded display. Syne UI.

**Primitive:** `kaleido-fold` (60deg, 0.88, 520ms). **Enter:** `wobble-land`.

**Anti-slop:** HitCounter, Comic Sans, infinite kaleidoscope loop, three icon cards.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Masthead, name, street.
