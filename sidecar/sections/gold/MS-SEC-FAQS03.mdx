## Promise (buyer-facing)

HELM is a mid-page **FAQ** that reads as a zebra salon, not a help center. One question. It punches from a crop to the full frame.

**Why use it:**
- The punch is the design. The board never says crop
- Black and white only. No third color
- Sits after Helix, Vertex, or Terra without copying Docket, Veil, or Optic

## Design System

Stage `#111111`. Ink `#F5F5F5`. Inter only.

**Primitive:** `crop-punch` (inset 18%, 320ms). **Enter:** `hard-cut`. Matching clip units `%` to `%`.

**Anti-slop:** accordion kit, rave strobe, tiny click labels.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Masthead, punched question, answer, street.
