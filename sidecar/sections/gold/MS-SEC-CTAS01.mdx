## Promise (buyer-facing)

DEW is a mid-page **close** that reads as a list, not a launch. One headline. One place. One button. Visitors do not pick between three pills. They hold until the name is taken.

**Why use it:**
- The close is a single verb, not a kit
- Hold-to-confirm is the design. The board never says hold
- Every line is a slot for your house
- Sits after Acne Secret, Aether, or STILL without copying their film
- Built for rooms that take names quietly

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **DEW** - mid-page **waitlist / CTA** (not a hero, not an email bar). Stage `#F4EFE6`. Ink `#2C241C`. Accent `#8C6A4A` unused as fill. Newsreader display. Figtree UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** Acne Secret restraint × Aether air × Aesop paper. Warm field. Hard-ish button (2px). No spa photograph. No blob.

**Design language:** `organic`  
**Token theme:** `wellness-warm`  
**Motion primitive:** `hold-to-confirm` (850ms, linear fill, cancel on release)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `hem-lift`. Cloth lifts from the hem. CSS clip `inset(0% 0% 100% 0%)` → `inset(0% 0% 0% 0%)`. Same units. Land ~1100ms. Interaction after land. Reduced motion skips enter.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Newsreader italic, `clamp(1.85rem, 3.4vw, 2.35rem)`
- Headline: Newsreader 400, `clamp(2.6rem, 8vw, 5.25rem)`, tracking `-0.035em`
- Period: Figtree, ~17px, muted, leading 1.55
- Place / meta / button: Figtree, 10–11px, uppercase tracking `0.18–0.22em`

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#F4EFE6` | Your paper |
| Ink | `#2C241C` | Your type |
| Muted | `#6E6458` | Period |
| Line | `#E4D8C8` | Rules if needed |
| Accent | `#8C6A4A` | Hairline only if you add one |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** three equal pills, Mailchimp email + Subscribe, App Store badge row, blob / clip-path organic button, stock spa or flower photo, Get Started on a gradient banner, exploding waitlist input.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Dew must not become |
|----|---------------------|
| Collect UI CTA wall | Dual pills, Download App, Get started |
| Tally | Proof blotter. Dew is a close |
| Issue | Gray board, turning sheet. Dew is paper and one hold |
| Phobia | Fleeing fields. Dew does not play |

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** hold-to-confirm. After land, pointer or Space/Enter held for 850ms fills the button from the left. Release before 850ms returns the fill. At 850ms the label becomes On the list and stays. Reduced motion: one click.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `hem-lift`. Whole board lifts from the hem. No caption.

**Engine:** CSS clip enter. Interval fill (32ms) with a timeout snap, same pattern as Tally's count (rAF throttles under CDP).

**Reduced motion:** full board at rest. Click confirms. No hem. No fill travel.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used. This is not a scroll-narrative section.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: rest + single click
- Contrast on cream ≥ 4.5:1
- Keyboard: Space / Enter hold (or click when reduced)
- Semantic `<section>`, `<h2>`, one `<button>`
- `aria-pressed` after confirm, `aria-live="polite"` on the control
- Label over the fill is clipped, not a second meaning

## Performance Notes

- No video, no WebGL, no extra kits
- One hold timer at a time
