## Promise (buyer-facing)

VEIL is a mid-page **FAQ** that reads as a night house, not a help center. One question owns the plate. The answer starts in haze, then clears.

**Why use it:**
- The haze is the design. The board never says blur
- Stage is pigment violet. Champagne is the jewel
- Questions are slots for your house
- Sits after Revel, Sable, or Dopamine without copying Port

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **VEIL** - mid-page **faq**. Stage `#12081C`. Ink `#EDE6F7`. Accent `#E8D5A3`. Fraunces italic display. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** nightlife salon. Velvet dark, one jewel light. Not a purple mesh.

**Design language:** `velvet-nocturne` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `ultraviolet-silk`  
**Motion primitive:** `perfume-haze` (8px blur, scale 1.04, 900ms)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `silk-slip`. Land ~1320ms. Haze after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Fraunces italic 500
- Question: Fraunces italic `clamp(2.4rem, 8.5vw, 5.6rem)`
- Answer: Outfit ~18px

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#12081C` | Your cloth |
| Ink | `#EDE6F7` | Your silk |
| Muted | `#A090C0` | Answer |
| Accent | `#E8D5A3` | Champagne |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** accordion kit, three FAQ columns, purple glow mesh, tiny click labels that swap the headline.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Veil must not become |
|----|----------------------|
| Docket | Clerk accordion. Veil is one question, haze |
| Port | Spotlight nights. Veil is a question that clears |

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** perfume-haze. After land, the answer blurs then clears once. Restage on each question.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `silk-slip`.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS filter/transform haze.

**Reduced motion:** first question at rest, answer sharp. Wheel is not captured.

**Pin-until-complete:** yes. Wheel / trackpad / touch / arrows. Hairline `--pin-p`. Index is readout, not a hit target.
