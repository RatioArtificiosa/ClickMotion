## Promise (buyer-facing)

WITNESS is a mid-page **statements section**. A name is chosen. The line takes the board. Visitors do not skim three avatar cards. They sit with one voice.

## Design System

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** magazine pull quote × SSENSE restraint × Helix board manners. Paper, not glass.

**Design language:** `luxury-editorial`  
**Token theme:** `fashion-ink-light`  
**Motion primitive:** `card-takeover` (crossfade 480ms, ease `[0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]`)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `drop-drape` (elegant). Duration 900ms, stagger 90ms. Names and quote land, then a name click still takes the plate.

**Anti-slop hard ban:** circular avatars, 5-star rows, logo marquee as proof, three equal quote cards, Inter italics on a gray tile.

## Expected Output

A single React section. Index of names. One quote owns the plate. No portraits required.
