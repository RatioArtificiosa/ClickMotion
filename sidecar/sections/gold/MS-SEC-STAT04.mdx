## Promise (buyer-facing)

BOLT is a mid-page **stats** board that reads as an optical press, not a ticker kit. One figure. A second ink knocks in.

**Why use it:**
- The knockout is the design. The board never says duotone
- Gallery white. Black only. Vibration is the wow
- Sits after Helix, Vertex, or Terra without copying Optic, Helm, or Press

## Design System

Stage `#F5F5F5`. Ink `#111111`. Inter only. No third color.

**Primitive:** `duotone-knockout` (300ms). **Enter:** `shear-slice`.

**Anti-slop:** number ticker, rave strobe, third color, sequin burst clone.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Masthead, figure, name, street.
