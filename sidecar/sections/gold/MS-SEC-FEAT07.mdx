## Promise (buyer-facing)

NIX is a mid-page **features** section that reads as an ice resin shop, not a bento. One object at a time. One bubble grows in the block, then stays.

**Why use it:**
- The bubble is the design. The board never says loom
- Stage is ice. White is the catch
- Object names are slots
- Sits after Prism, Mirage, or Verve without copying Cast

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **NIX** - mid-page **features**. Stage `#B8E4F0`. Ink `#0A2030`. Accent `#FFFFFF`. Unbounded display. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** design-object plastic. Translucent ice. Not cyan neon.

**Design language:** `resin-object` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `resin-ice`  
**Motion primitive:** `bubble-loom` (scale from 0, 560ms). One bubble. Not a soda field.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `fade-hold`. Land ~1400ms. Bubble after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Unbounded 700
- Object: Unbounded 800 `clamp(2.8rem, 10vw, 6.4rem)`
- Period: Outfit ~18px

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#B8E4F0` | Your ice |
| Ink | `#0A2030` | Navy |
| Accent | `#FFFFFF` | Bubble |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** three equal tiles, soda particle field, cheap acrylic neon, amber clone of Cast.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Nix must not become |
|----|---------------------|
| Cast | Amber lid. Nix is ice and one bubble |
| Kiln | Wet drip. Nix is a trapped air |

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** bubble-loom. One circle grows in the ice once per object.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `fade-hold`.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS transform bubble.

**Reduced motion:** first object at rest, bubble already there. Wheel is not captured.

**Pin-until-complete:** yes. Wheel / trackpad / touch / arrows. Hairline `--pin-p`. Index is readout, not a hit target.
