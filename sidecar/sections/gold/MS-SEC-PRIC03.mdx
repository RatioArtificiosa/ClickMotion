## Promise (buyer-facing)

GILDA is a mid-page **pricing** board that reads as a makeup book, not three kit cards. One sitting. Empty ghosts behind. The plate shuffles 3 degrees and lands.

**Why use it:**
- The shuffle is the design. The board never says deck
- Stage is lacquer. Ivory is the highlight
- Sits after Revel, Verve, or Dopamine without copying Rouge or Cairn

## Design System

Stage `#C41E3A`. Ink `#FFF5F0`. Syne display. Outfit UI.

**Primitive:** `stack-shuffle` (480ms, 3deg). **Enter:** `hop-in`.

**Anti-slop:** three equal pricing cards, lipstick swipe clone, Get Started.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Masthead, one plate, street.
