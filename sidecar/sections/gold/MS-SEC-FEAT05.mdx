## Promise (buyer-facing)

CAST is a mid-page **features section** that reads as one resin object, not a product grid. The block starts on its edge, then opens. Click to close. Hover another name to open the next.

**Why use it:**
- The lid is the design. The board never says flip
- Stage is amber. White is the catch
- Object names are slots
- Sits after Prism, Mirage, or Verve without copying their film

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **CAST** - mid-page **features**. Stage `#E8A04A`. Ink `#1A0E04`. Catch `#FFF6E8`. Unbounded display. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** design-object still. Radius 28px on the object. Not cheap acrylic.

**Design language:** `resin-object` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `resin-amber`  
**Motion primitive:** `flip-clamshell` (rotateX 88 to 0, 540ms, perspective 1000)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `fade-hold`. Land ~1400ms. Lid after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Unbounded 700
- Object: Unbounded 800, `clamp(3rem, 10vw, 6.4rem)`
- Period: Outfit ~18px

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#E8A04A` | Your resin |
| Ink | `#1A0E04` | Your dark |
| Muted | `#6A3A12` | Period |
| Accent | `#FFF6E8` | Catch |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** three product cards, 3D kit, rainbow fill, toy plastic.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Cast must not become |
|----|------------------------|
| Kiln | Wet drip. Cast is a lid |
| Prism | Glass hero. Cast is one object |
| Tilt | Chrome bead. Cast is amber |

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** flip-clamshell. After land, rotateX 88deg opens to 0. Click toggles. Next object restages the lid.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `fade-hold`. Opacity only.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS 3D transform.

**Reduced motion:** open rest. Instant object swap.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used.
