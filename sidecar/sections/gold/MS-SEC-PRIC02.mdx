## Promise (buyer-facing)

CAIRN is a mid-page **pricing** board that reads as a jewelry case, not a SaaS table. Three sittings. The kept one takes a carnelian seal.

**Why use it:**
- The seal is the design. The board never says stamp
- Stage is oxblood black. Carnelian is the wax
- Sitting names are slots
- Sits after Prism, Mirage, or Elyse without copying Facet

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** wax-seal. The disc stamps only on the kept sitting. Other sittings stay unsealed.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `iris-open`. Matching clip units `%` to `%`.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS scale stamp.

**Reduced motion:** first sitting at rest. Seal static if Keep is shown. Wheel is not captured.

**Pin-until-complete:** yes. Wheel / trackpad / touch / arrows. Hairline `--pin-p`. Index is readout, not a hit target.
