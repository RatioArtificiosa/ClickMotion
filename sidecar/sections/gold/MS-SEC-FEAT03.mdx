## Promise (buyer-facing)

KERN is a mid-page **features section** that reads as a pasted poster, not a bento. Two words slam from opposite sides and lock as overprint. Visitors do not scan six icon tiles. They take a wall.

**Why use it:**
- The lockup is the capability. Crop is the layout
- Two inks only. Vermilion multiplies through black
- Hover or click a sheet. The next pair collides
- Sits after Revel, Helix, or Verve without copying the helix
- Built for houses that paste a season, not a grid

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **KERN** - mid-page **kinetic poster** (not a hero, not Issue, not a type kit). Stage `#F2EFE4`. Ink `#111111`. Accent `#FF3B00` as the second ink only. Archivo Black display. Inter period. IBM Plex Mono labels.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** experimental poster × Helix crop × a wheatpaste wall. Two type roles. No glow. No mesh.

**Design language:** `kinetic-poster` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `poster-day`  
**Motion primitive:** `type-collision` (120px from each side, 380ms, ease `[0.55, 0, 0.15, 1]`)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `offset-print`. Misregister `-7px / 7px` that locks. Land ~700ms. Collision after land. Reduced motion skips enter and shows the composed lockup.

**Typography:**
- Collision words: Archivo Black, `clamp(4.6rem, 22vw, 13.25rem)`, tracking `-0.07em`, line `0.74`, uppercase
- Period: Inter ~15px, muted
- Masthead / indexes / place: IBM Plex Mono, 10–11px, uppercase tracking `0.2em`

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#F2EFE4` | Your paper |
| Ink | `#111111` | First ink |
| Muted | `#3A3A3A` | Period |
| Accent | `#FF3B00` | Second ink |
| Overprint | multiply blend | Do not add a third ink |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** three equal cards, icon bento, shiny H1, glow on every word, RGB chromatic-aberration kit, school-bus rave, mesh, Issue gray board clone, Helix orbit.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Kern must not become |
|----|----------------------|
| Issue | Gray spread that turns. Kern is a pasted collision |
| Atlas | Swiss name rise. Kern overprints two inks |
| Helix | Spatial gallery. Kern is one lockup on a wall |
| Magic UI | Split-flap / shiny text. Kern is print |

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` poster. Overflow hidden. Type is allowed to crop.

1. Masthead: Kern left, sheet index right
2. Lockup: two words, same cell, opposite slam, multiply overprint
3. Foot: job name, place, period, three indexes

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** type-collision. After land, the pair slams 120px from each side in 380ms. Hover or click an index (or arrows) restages and slams again. Final pose is a slight misregister. Not a marquee. Not three cards rising.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `offset-print`. CSS translate. Interaction starts after land.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS collision.

**Reduced motion:** composed rest. Words already overlapped. Instant sheet swap. No slam.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used. This is not a scroll-narrative section.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: composed lockup, instant swap
- Contrast on cream ≥ 4.5:1 for ink. Vermilion is the second ink, not the only text
- Keyboard: sheet tabs, arrows after land
- Semantic `<section>`, `role="tablist"`, `aria-live="polite"` on the lockup

## Performance Notes

- No video, no WebGL, no Lottie
- One slam at a time. `will-change` only on the two words
