## Promise (buyer-facing)

NORI is a mid-page **testimonials** board that reads as a pistachio shop, not a quote carousel. One voice. The line squashes once, then sits.

**Why use it:**
- The jelly is the design. The board never says squash
- Stage is pistachio. Cherry is one candy
- Quotes and names are slots
- Sits after Revel, Verve, or Dopamine without copying Port or Rouge

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** Stage `#D8E8C8`. Ink `#1A2E14`. Accent `#E23B5A`. Syne display. Outfit UI.

**Design language:** `candy-couture` · **Theme:** `pistachio-salon` · **Primitive:** `jelly-morph` (1.08 / 0.92)

**Enter:** `pop-in`. Land ~880ms.

**Anti-slop:** avatar cards, emoji, three equal quotes, Comic bounce as identity.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pinned stage. Masthead, quote, name, street.
