## Promise (buyer-facing)

OPTIC is a mid-page **features section** that reads as a gallery wall, not a stripe kit. Black and white only. The bands move eight pixels, once. Never a loop.

**Why use it:**
- The vibration is the design. The board never says moire
- No third color
- Room names are slots
- Sits after Helix, Vertex, or Terra Nova without copying their film

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **OPTIC** - mid-page **features**. Stage `#F5F5F5`. Ink `#111111`. Inter only.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** optical gallery. Hard black white. Size is the craft.

**Design language:** `op-signal` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `moire-gallery`  
**Motion primitive:** `moire-shift` (8px, 900ms, one cycle)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `hard-cut`. Land ~200ms. Shift after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Inter 800, uppercase
- Room: Inter 800, `clamp(3rem, 11vw, 7.4rem)`, uppercase
- Period: Inter 500 ~17px

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#F5F5F5` | Stay near white |
| Ink | `#111111` | Stay near black |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** third color, infinite loop, rave strobe, rainbow op-art, bento.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Optic must not become |
|----|------------------------|
| Helix | Orbiting works. Optic is rooms |
| Kern | Two-ink poster. Optic is black and white |
| Atlas | Swiss systems. Optic is a gallery |

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` stage. Bands under the type.

1. Masthead: Optic left, index right
2. Room name, period
3. Foot: place, three rooms

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** moire-shift. After land, overlay translates 8px in 900ms, once. Restage on each room. Never loop.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `hard-cut`.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS keyframes, one cycle.

**Reduced motion:** static bands. Instant room swap.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used.
