## Promise (buyer-facing)

CLEAR is a **footer** that reads as a close, not a sitemap. One desk holds the proof. Three house links. One seat request. Visitors do not hunt twelve columns. They leave knowing the books are open.

**Why use it:**
- Completeness without a dump: brand, desks, nav, legal, one verb
- The desk rail is the motion. Accent is a hairline
- Every place and period is a slot for your house
- Sits after Orbit, Axiom, or Nexus without copying their film
- Built for rooms that clear, not announce

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **CLEAR** - page **footer / close** (not a hero, not Dopamine). Stage `#0B0F14`. Ink `#F4F7FA`. Accent `#7C9CFF` as the active desk hairline only. Inter display. IBM Plex Mono labels.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** Stripe trust × Orbit night × a clearing blotter. Quiet chrome. No fashion scramble.

**Design language:** `calm-fintech`  
**Token theme:** `orbit-trust-dark`  
**Motion primitive:** `desk-ink` (ink 220ms, hairline 280ms, origin left)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `stamp-settle`. Type settles 14px / 0.988. Land ~980ms. Interaction after land. Reduced motion skips enter.

**Typography:**
- Place: Inter 400, `clamp(2.2rem, 5.6vw, 4.1rem)`, tracking `-0.045em`
- Period: Inter ~15px, muted
- Masthead / desks index / nav / CTA / legal: IBM Plex Mono, 10–11px, uppercase tracking `0.18em`

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**

**Anti-slop hard ban:** four-column sitemap, Mailchimp newsletter under a gradient banner, App Store row, Dopamine scramble/fashion clone, rising column kit, Orbit blue as a fill.

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** desk-ink. After land, hover or click a desk (or arrows). The proof restages. The accent hairline `scaleX` follows the active desk. Not a count. Not four columns rising.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `stamp-settle`. CSS cubic standing in for the letterpress. Interaction starts after land.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS hairline. State swap for copy.

**Reduced motion:** rest at Charlotte. Instant desk swap. Hairline already full on the active. No settle.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used. This is not a scroll-narrative section.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: rest, instant swap
- Contrast on `#0B0F14` ≥ 4.5:1
- Keyboard: desk tabs, arrows after land
- Semantic `<footer>`, `<h2>`, `role="tablist"`
- `aria-live="polite"` on the place
- Accent is a 2px hairline, not the only meaning

## Performance Notes

- No video, no WebGL, no Lottie, no scramble
- One active desk at a time
