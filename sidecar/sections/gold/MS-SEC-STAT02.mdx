## Promise (buyer-facing)

PRESS is a mid-page **stats** board that reads as a night poster, not a dashboard. One figure. It arrives too big, then slams to reading size.

**Why use it:**
- The crash is the design. The board never says scale
- Stage is ink black. School-bus yellow is the signal
- Figures are slots
- Sits after Revel, Helix, or Verve without copying Kern

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **PRESS** - mid-page **stats**. Stage `#0A0A0A`. Ink `#F5F0E6`. Accent `#FFE600`. Archivo Black display. Inter UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** experimental poster. Colliding type scale. Not a mesh.

**Design language:** `kinetic-poster` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `poster-night`  
**Motion primitive:** `scale-crash` (from 2.4, 380ms)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `offset-print`. Land ~700ms. Crash after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: IBM Plex Mono tracked
- Figure: Archivo Black `clamp(5.4rem, 28vw, 18rem)`
- Name: Archivo Black uppercase

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#0A0A0A` | Night paper |
| Ink | `#F5F0E6` | Ivory |
| Accent | `#FFE600` | Your signal |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** four stat cards, bouncing ticker, counting kit, RGB split.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Press must not become |
|----|----------------------|
| Tally | Desk count. Press is a slam |
| Kern | Two-word collision. Press is one figure |

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** scale-crash. Giant type slams to reading size once per figure.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `offset-print`.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS transform crash.

**Reduced motion:** first figure at rest. Wheel is not captured.

**Pin-until-complete:** yes. Wheel / trackpad / touch / arrows. Hairline `--pin-p`. Index is readout, not a hit target.
