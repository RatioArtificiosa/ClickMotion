## Promise (buyer-facing)

HEAT is a mid-page **close** that reads as a stamped menu, not a gold filter. The line hits oversized, then locks. Click the table to stamp the button.

**Why use it:**
- The hit is the design. The board never says foil
- Stage is sand. Leaf gold is the first ink
- Every line is a slot
- Sits after Elyse, Nomad, or Meridian without copying their film

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **HEAT** - mid-page **waitlist / CTA**. Stage `#E8C97A`. Ink `#2A1808`. Leaf `#C9A227`. Cinzel display. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** desert couture, hot foil. Radius 4px. No crypto foil.

**Design language:** `solar-gilt` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `desert-leaf`  
**Motion primitive:** `foil-stamp` (scale 1.18, rotate -4deg, 320ms)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `fade-hold`. Land ~1400ms. Stamp after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Cinzel 700, uppercase tracking `0.12em`
- Headline: Cinzel 700, `clamp(2.6rem, 8.2vw, 5.6rem)`
- Period: Outfit ~17px

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**

**Anti-slop hard ban:** crypto foil, aurora gold, stock desert photo, Facet jewel clone.
