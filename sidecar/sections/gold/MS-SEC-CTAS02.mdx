## Promise (buyer-facing)

ROUGE is a mid-page **close** that reads as a lacquered room, not a launch bar. One headline. One street. One chair. The cream paints across the type. Visitors do not pick between three pills. They save a seat.

**Why use it:**
- The swipe is the design. The board never says swipe
- Stage is the lacquer. Cream is the second ink
- Every line is a slot for your house
- Sits after Revel, Verve, or Dopamine without copying their film
- Built for rooms that open at night

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **ROUGE** - mid-page **waitlist / CTA** (not a hero, not Dew, not a Mailchimp bar). Stage `#C41E3A`. Ink `#FFF5F0`. Gloss `#FFD6C8`. Syne display. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** couture lacquer × a salon door after eight. Hard-ish button (12px). No blob. No emoji.

**Design language:** `candy-couture` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `cherry-lacquer`  
**Motion primitive:** `lipstick-swipe` (`inset(0% 100% 0% 0%)` → `inset(0% 0% 0% 0%)`, 480ms, ease `[0.2, 0.12, 0.18, 1]`)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `pop-in`. Scale 0.92 / 28px. Land ~880ms. Lipstick after land. Reduced motion skips enter and shows the painted word.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Syne 700, `clamp(1.7rem, 3.2vw, 2.15rem)`
- Headline: Syne 800, `clamp(2.6rem, 8.4vw, 5.6rem)`, tracking `-0.045em`
- Period: Outfit ~17px, muted
- Place / meta / button: Outfit, 10–12px, uppercase tracking `0.16–0.2em`

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |

| Stage | `#C41E3A` | Your lacquer |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** three equal pills, Mailchimp email + Subscribe, App Store row, blob button, Dew hold clone, Get Started on a gradient, emoji, kit radius 999.

**Differentiation:**

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** lipstick-swipe. After land, cream paints the headline left to right in 480ms. Hover the button to paint it. Click to keep it and change the label. Same clip units `%` → `%`.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `pop-in`. CSS standing in for the spring. Interaction after land.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS clip.

**Reduced motion:** painted rest. One click confirms. No travel.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used.
