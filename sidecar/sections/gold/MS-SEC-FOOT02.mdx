## Promise (buyer-facing)

BRINE is a **footer** that reads as a recovered arcade room, not a link dump. One glacier tube. Three rooms.

**Why use it:**
- The tube is the design. The board never says neon
- Stage is ice silver. One tube, not a city
- Sits after Verve, Actually!, or Dopamine without copying Clear or Tilt

## Design System

Stage `#E4EEF4`. Ink `#1A2330`. Accent `#7ED0E8`. Unbounded display. Syne UI.

**Primitive:** `neon-ignite` (3 flickers, 70ms, then hold). **Enter:** `pop-in`.

**Anti-slop:** HitCounter, Comic Sans, four gray columns, neon grid.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Wordmark with one tube, room name, links, close.
