## Promise (buyer-facing)

HOLT is a mid-page **contact** that reads as a commission desk, not a SaaS form. The form docks from the right. Send snaps the button home.

**Why use it:**
- The snap is the design. The board never says magnet
- Stage is amber. White is the catch
- Sits after Prism, Mirage, or Verve without copying Cast or Ember

## Design System

Stage `#E8A04A`. Ink `#1A0E04`. Unbounded display. Outfit UI.

**Primitive:** `magnet-snap` (40px, 160ms). **Enter:** `hang-rail`.

**Anti-slop:** floating labels, three-column form, neon plastic toy, blob field.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh`. Masthead, headline, form.
