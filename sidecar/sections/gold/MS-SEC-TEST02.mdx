## Promise (buyer-facing)

PORT is a mid-page **proof section** that reads as a club after midnight, not a quote carousel. One line from a real night. Three names. The light follows the hand, then parks.

**Why use it:**
- The pool of light is the design. The board never says spotlight
- Stage is velvet. Champagne is the lamp
- Names and cities are slots
- Sits after Revel, Sable, or Dopamine without copying their film

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **PORT** - mid-page **testimonials**. Stage `#1A0A12`. Ink `#F6E8EE`. Champagne `#E8D5A3`. Fraunces italic. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** nightlife salon. Soft radius on nothing. No neon city.

**Design language:** `velvet-nocturne` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `club-bordeaux`  
**Motion primitive:** `spotlight-park` (follow, then 420ms park)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `silk-slip`. 20px. Land ~1320ms. Spotlight after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Fraunces italic 600
- Quote: Fraunces italic `clamp(2.2rem, 6.4vw, 4.4rem)`
- Names: Fraunces italic ~17px
- Place / city: Outfit, 10px, uppercase

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#1A0A12` | Your velvet |
| Ink | `#F6E8EE` | Your rose |
| Muted | `#C4A0B0` | Idle names |
| Accent | `#E8D5A3` | The lamp |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** quote carousel, five stars, headshots in a row, neon grid, Witness takeover clone.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Port must not become |
|----|------------------------|
| Witness | Statement takes the plate. Port keeps one lamp |
| Rouge | Lacquer close. Port is proof |
| Sable | Fashion hero. Port is a mid-page night |
