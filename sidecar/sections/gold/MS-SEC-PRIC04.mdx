## Promise (buyer-facing)

DUNE is a mid-page **pricing** board that reads as three nights, not three kit cards. One sheen across the gilt price.

**Why use it:**
- The sweep is the design. The board never says vitrine
- Stage is gilt sand. Leaf gold is the price
- Sits after Elyse, Nomad, or Meridian without copying Heat, Sol, or Gilda

## Design System

Stage `#E8C97A`. Ink `#2A1808`. Accent `#C9A227`. Cinzel display. Outfit UI.

**Primitive:** `vitrine-sweep` (900ms, 24deg). **Enter:** `match-cut`.

**Anti-slop:** three equal pricing cards, crypto foil, shiny-text identity, dinner clone.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Masthead, night, gilt price, street.
