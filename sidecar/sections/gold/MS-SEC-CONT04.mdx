## Promise (buyer-facing)

REED is a mid-page **contact** that reads as a letterpress desk, not a SaaS form. You don't have to have it finished. The type slips two pixels, once.

**Why use it:**
- The slip is the design. The board never says chromatic
- Stage is night paper. One vermilion seal
- Sits after Revel, Helix, or Verve without copying Mark, Quill, or Holt

## Design System

Stage `#1A1A1A`. Ink `#F5F0E8`. Accent `#E23B2A`. Newsreader display. Inter UI. IBM Plex Mono labels.

**Primitive:** `chromatic-slip` (2px, 200ms). **Enter:** `letterpress-crush`.

**Anti-slop:** floating labels, red website, matrix glitch, smoke-script clone.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh`. Masthead, headline, form.
