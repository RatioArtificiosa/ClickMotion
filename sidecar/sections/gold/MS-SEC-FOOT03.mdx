## Promise (buyer-facing)

VESPER is a **footer** that reads as a listening room, not a link dump. Three rooms. The name presses like velvet, once.

**Why use it:**
- The crush is the design. The board never says velvet
- Stage is pigment violet. Champagne is the jewel
- Sits after Revel, Sable, or Dopamine without copying Port, Veil, or Brine
