## Promise (buyer-facing)

MARK is a mid-page **close** that reads as a show poster, not a scramble kit. The title falls into place. One vermilion seal. One street.

**Why use it:**
- The cascade is the design. The board never says letters
- Stage is night paper. Red is the seal
- Every line is a slot
- Sits after Revel, Helix, or Verve without copying their film

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **MARK** - mid-page **waitlist / CTA**. Stage `#1A1A1A`. Ink `#F5F0E8`. Seal `#E23B2A`. Newsreader italic. Inter UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** fashion-week title. Square seal. No graffiti clutter.

**Design language:** `ink-riot` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `vermilion-colophon`  
**Motion primitive:** `glyph-cascade` (40px, stagger 22ms, 420ms)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `offset-print`. Land ~700ms. Cascade after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Newsreader italic 700
- Headline: Newsreader italic `clamp(3rem, 11vw, 7.2rem)`
- Period: Inter ~17px
- Button: Inter uppercase

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#1A1A1A` | Your night |
| Ink | `#F5F0E8` | Your paper |
| Muted | `#9A9488` | Period |
| Accent | `#E23B2A` | The seal |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** matrix scramble, tags everywhere, Kern collision clone, three pills.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Mark must not become |
|----|------------------------|
| Kern | Two words collide. Mark is one falling title |
| Rouge | Lacquer paint. Mark is ink |
| Helix | Gallery orbit. Mark is a poster close |
