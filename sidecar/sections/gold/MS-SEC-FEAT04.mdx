## Promise (buyer-facing)

KILN is a mid-page **features section** that reads as a pottery shop, not a bento. One piece at a time. A wet highlight drops after the name lands.

**Why use it:**
- The drip is the design. The board never says glaze
- Stage is celadon. White is the catchlight
- Piece names are slots
- Sits after Elyse, STILL, or Aether without copying their film

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **KILN** - mid-page **features**. Stage `#C5D5C8`. Ink `#1C2E28`. Catch `#F4FBF6`. Fraunces display. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** wet glaze, object photography. Generous radius on the drip only. Not mint SaaS.

**Design language:** `ceramic-gloss` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `celadon-wet`  
**Motion primitive:** `glaze-drip` (28px, spring-like settle)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `silk-slip`. Land ~1320ms. Drip after land.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Fraunces 600
- Piece: Fraunces `clamp(3.2rem, 12vw, 8rem)`
- Period: Outfit ~18px
- Index: Fraunces italic

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#C5D5C8` | Your clay |
| Ink | `#1C2E28` | Your dark |
| Muted | `#4A6358` | Period |
| Accent | `#F4FBF6` | Wet white |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** three equal tiles, leaf particles, wellness beige kit, terrazzo clipart.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Kiln must not become |

**Signature (one):** glaze-drip. After land, the catchlight drops 28px and settles. Restage on each piece.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `silk-slip`.

**Engine:** CSS enter. CSS transform drip.

**Reduced motion:** drip at rest. Instant piece swap.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used.
