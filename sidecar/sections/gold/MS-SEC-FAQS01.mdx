## Promise (buyer-facing)

DOCKET is a mid-page **questions section** that reads like counsel, not a help center. One line is open. The answer stages in by height. Visitors do not hunt a chevron. They read a docket.

**Why use it:**
- Your FAQ feels written, not generated
- One open line keeps the page calm
- Every question and paragraph is a slot for your house
- Sits after a fintech hero or after Ledger pricing
- Built for desks that answer in writing

## Design System

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** Linear density × Stripe calm × a clerk's docket. Dark paper. No glass.

**Design language:** `calm-fintech`  
**Token theme:** `orbit-trust-dark`  
**Motion primitive:** `accordion-stage` (height 420ms, ease `[0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94]`, ink 200ms)

**Anti-slop hard ban:** gray chevrons, plus/minus icons, card stacks, purple accordions, Intercom help-center chrome, three-column FAQ grids.

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** accordion-stage. Only one item open. Click the open line to close. Reduced motion: instant height.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `click-in` (aggressive clerk). Duration 280ms, stagger 48ms, ease `[0.2, 0.12, 0.18, 1]`. Lines land closed, then the first answer stages open. Hover inks a closed line. Click after land.

## Expected Output

A single React section. Dark stage. Typographic list. Height-staged answers. No chevron kit.
