## Promise (buyer-facing)

QUILL is a mid-page **features** board that reads as a print shop, not a bento. One word. It arrives as smoke, then locks.

**Why use it:**
- The smoke is the design. The board never says blur
- Stage is raw paper. Black is the only ink
- Sits after Revel, Helix, or Verve without copying Mark or Kern

## Design System

Stage `#F4F0E6`. Ink `#111111`. Newsreader italic display. Inter UI. IBM Plex Mono colophon.

**Primitive:** `smoke-script` (6px, 0.18em, 800ms). **Enter:** `offset-print`.

**Anti-slop:** graffiti clutter, RGB split, three tiles.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Masthead, smoked word, period, street.
