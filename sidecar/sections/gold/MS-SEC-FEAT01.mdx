## Promise (buyer-facing)

ATLAS is a mid-page **features section** that reads as a site drawing, not a bento. One system holds the plate. The name rises from a mask. Visitors do not scan six icon tiles. They sit with a figure and three lines of paper.

**Why use it:**
- Your capabilities feel specified, not marketed
- Type does the motion. No cards, no icons, no glow
- Every name, figure, and row is a slot for your yard
- Sits after Terra Nova, Apex, or Nexus without copying their film
- Built for climate, hardware, and desks that ship from a drawing

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **ATLAS** - mid-page **systems / features** (not a hero, not a bento). Gallery white `#FAFAFA`. Ink `#111111`. Radius 0. Grotesk display. Mono labels. Hairline rules.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** Apple marketing spec restraint × modernist grid × a one-line electrical drawing. Dry paper. No glass.

**Design language:** `swiss-minimal`  
**Token theme:** `swiss-light`  
**Motion primitive:** `text-split-reveal` (word mask, 700ms, stagger 28ms, ease `[0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]`)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `paper-slide`. Duration 640ms, stagger 60ms. Interaction after land (~900ms). Reduced motion skips enter.

**Typography:**
- Display name: Inter 400, `clamp(2.35rem, 6.4vw, 4.75rem)`, tracking `-0.045em`, line-height `1.02`
- Figure: Inter 400, `clamp(2.35rem, 6.8vw, 5.35rem)`, tabular-nums, tracking `-0.05em`
- Period: Inter 13px, muted
- Labels / masthead / CTA: IBM Plex Mono, 10–11px, uppercase tracking `0.14–0.22em`

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#FAFAFA` | Your board |
| Ink | `#111111` | Your type |
| Muted | `#6B6B6B` | Captions |
| Line | `#E8E8E8` | Rules |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** icon + title + blurb bento, three equal rounded cards, sage glass leftover from Terra, cyan quantum leftover from Apex, purple mesh, shiny H1, pill docks, Magic UI / v0 feature grid.

**Differentiation:**
| vs | Atlas must not become |
|----|------------------------|
| Kit features | Six icons in a bento |
| Witness | Quotes and names. Atlas is a spec plate |
| Ledger | Pin and seats. Atlas clicks a system. No pin |
| Docket | Questions. Atlas is capabilities |

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** text-split-reveal. Each word sits in overflow hidden and rises `y: 110% → 0`. Duration 700ms. Stagger 28ms. Retriggers when the system changes.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `paper-slide`. Interaction (index, rail, CTA press) starts after land.

**Engine:** Motion (Framer lineage).

**Reduced motion:** names appear whole. No mask rise. Instant plate swap.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used. This is not a scroll-narrative section.

## Video / Media Integration

None. Paper board. No background film.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: whole names, no rise
- Contrast on white ≥ 4.5:1
- Keyboard: index and rail are buttons
- Semantic `<section>`, `<h2>`, `<dl>`, rail `role="tablist"`
- `aria-live="polite"` on the display plate

## Performance Notes

- No video, no WebGL, no extra kits
- One enter spring of slides, one split per change
- Four plates, only the active one mounted
