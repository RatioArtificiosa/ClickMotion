## What we take (craft, not composition)

- **One job.** The boards that feel expensive are a headline, air, and one verb. “Built for daily wear.” / Renance / Alex Monroe / VOIR.
- **Photography as photography.** Grass, interior grain, triptych stills. Not a mesh, not a gummy-bear pile.
- **Type does the selling.** Serif editorial or one heavy sans. Not a gradient H1 with an orb.
- **Quiet close.** One waitlist line beats store-badge rows and dual chat/call pills.
- **Motion is a primitive, not a costume.** Framer rewards scroll-transition and hover. Our pin-until-complete law already covers scroll narrative. Do not paste their scroll toys onto Dew.
