## Promise (buyer-facing)

FACET is a mid-page **close** that reads as a jewelry case, not a checkout bar. One line. One street. One stone. The room opens from the center. The gilt follows the hand, then rests.

**Why use it:**
- The swing is the design. The board never says swing
- Stage is the case. Gold is the stone
- Every line is a slot for your house
- Sits after Prism, Mirage, or Elyse without copying their film

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **FACET** - mid-page **waitlist / CTA**. Stage `#0E0F12`. Ink `#F4F1EA`. Gilt `#C9A24A`. Cinzel display. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** high jewelry vitrine. One gem. Hard corners (2px). No rainbow chrome.

**Design language:** `jewel-chrome` (wow register)

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** jewel-swing. After land, the button pulls with the pointer, capped at 10px and 8deg. Leave to rest. Confirm parks it.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `iris-open`. CSS clip `%` to `%`.

**Engine:** CSS enter. Transform swing.

**Reduced motion:** open rest. No swing. One click confirms.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used.
