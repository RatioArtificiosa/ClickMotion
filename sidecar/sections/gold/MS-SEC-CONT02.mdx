## Promise (buyer-facing)

EMBER is a mid-page **contact** that reads as a kiln note, not a SaaS form. Hairline fields. Send pours cream glaze from the top.

**Why use it:**
- The pour is the design. The board never says lacquer
- Stage is oxblood. Cream is the catch
- Labels are slots
- Sits after Elyse, STILL, or Aether without copying Kiln

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **EMBER** - mid-page **contact**. Stage `#5C1218`. Ink `#F6E6D8`. Accent `#E8C4A8`. Fraunces display. Outfit UI.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** wet ceramic, object photography. Generous radius on the send only.

**Design language:** `ceramic-gloss` (wow register)  
**Token theme:** `oxblood-glaze`  
**Motion primitive:** `lacquer-pour` (clip from bottom, 780ms)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `silk-slip`. Land ~1320ms. Pour on send.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Fraunces 600
- Headline: Fraunces `clamp(2.4rem, 6.5vw, 4.4rem)`
- Fields: Outfit ~17px
