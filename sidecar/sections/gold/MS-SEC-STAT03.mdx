## Promise (buyer-facing)

PEARL is a mid-page **stats** board that reads as a jewelry case, not a ticker. One figure. Twelve sequins leave once.

**Why use it:**
- The burst is the design. The board never says sequin
- Stage is the case. Gilt is the stone
- Sits after Prism, Mirage, or Elyse without copying Facet or Press

## Design System

Stage `#0E0F12`. Metal `#D8D5D0`. Gilt `#C9A24A`. Cinzel display. Outfit UI.

**Primitive:** `sequin-burst` (12 dots, 48px, 420ms). **Enter:** `pin-drop`.

**Anti-slop:** emoji confetti, number ticker kit, four stat cards.

## Layout Structure

`100dvh` pin. Masthead, figure, street.
