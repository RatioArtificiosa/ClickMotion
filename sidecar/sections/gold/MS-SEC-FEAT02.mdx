## Promise (buyer-facing)

ISSUE is a mid-page **features section** that reads as a magazine spread, not a bento. One story holds the sheet. The crop opens the board. Then a contents line turns the paper. Visitors do not scan six icon tiles. They sit with a room and three lines.

**Why use it:**
- Your capabilities feel edited, not marketed
- The sheet turns. No cards, no icons, no orbit
- Every name, figure, and row is a slot for your book
- Sits after Revel, Sable, or Helix without copying the helix
- Built for fashion, press, and desks that ship from a lookbook

## Design System

**Aesthetic Direction:** **ISSUE** - mid-page **capability spread** (not a hero, not Helix, not a bento). Board gray `#C3C3C3`. Ink `#1A1A1A`. Paper sheet `#D8D8D8`. Birthstone wordmark only. Inter for the book.

**Famous-UI craft direction (not a clone):** SSENSE contents page × Helix board gray × a press sheet that turns. Photography owns focus even when the photograph is type. No glass.

**Design language:** `luxury-editorial`  
**Token theme:** `editorial-board-gray`  
**Motion primitive:** `paper-pivot` (rotateX 8deg, 550ms, ease `[0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]`, origin bottom)

**Enter (support, once on view):** `crop-reveal`. Duration 860ms. Interaction after land (~1080ms). Reduced motion skips enter.

**Typography:**
- Wordmark: Birthstone, `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.4rem)`
- Display name: Inter 400, `clamp(2.5rem, 6.2vw, 4.85rem)`, tracking `-0.045em`
- Figure: Inter 400, `clamp(1.35rem, 2.8vw, 2.15rem)`, muted, tabular-nums
- Period: Inter 14px, muted
- Contents / folio / CTA: Inter, 10–15px

**Color tokens (defaults - replace freely):**
| Token | Default | Change to |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Stage | `#C3C3C3` | Your board |

**Anti-slop hard ban:** icon + title + blurb bento, three equal rounded cards, Helix orbit clone, Folio glass clone, Witness quote clone, shiny H1, pill docks, Magic UI / v0 feature grid.

## Motion Specification

**Signature (one):** paper-pivot. The sheet lifts `rotateX: 8deg → 0` from the bottom edge when the contents change. Duration 550ms. Retriggers on each story.

**Enter (support, once on view):** `crop-reveal`. The board opens from a 12% inset crop. Interaction (contents, folio, CTA) starts after land.

**Engine:** CSS for crop and pivot. Motion only for `prefers-reduced-motion` detection and land gating.

**Reduced motion:** full crop at rest. Instant sheet swap. No rotate.

**Pin-until-complete:** not used. This is not a scroll-narrative section.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: rest crop, no pivot
- Contrast on gray ≥ 4.5:1 for body
- Keyboard: contents and folio are buttons, arrows after land
- Semantic `<section>`, `<h2>`, `<dl>`, folio `role="tablist"`
- `aria-live="polite"` on the sheet

## Performance Notes

- No video, no WebGL, no extra kits
- One crop on view, one pivot per change
- One sheet mounted
