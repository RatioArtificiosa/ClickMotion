# Wow register (factory)

Optional second register. **Not the house default.** V1 house remains calm-fintech, luxury-editorial, swiss, cinematic, organic, brutalist, liquid-glass, Material You.

Wow is ultra-premium surprise: jewelry, nightlife, couture play, poster collision, wet ceramic, resin objects, calligraphy, gilt, optical art, recovered arcade as atelier.

Wow is **not** Magic UI. Aurora mesh, shiny H1, border beam, emoji confetti, purple glow, and rainbow glass still fail.

Cite `register: "wow"` language + matching wow theme + **one** wow signature primitive. Enter still comes from `ENTER.md`. Fintech and Swiss stay on the house register unless the operator explicitly asks for wow.

Index: languages below · themes in `token-themes.json` · primitives in `motion-primitives.json`.

## Mix law

- Wow language + wow theme + one wow primitive.
- Do not put wow on Ledger-class fintech unless the operator asks.
- Do not stack wow primitives (no jelly + strobe + sequin + chrome drip).
- Enter remains one recipe from `ENTER.md` (fun pool for candy/arcade: pop-in, hop-in, snap-elastic, wobble-land).
