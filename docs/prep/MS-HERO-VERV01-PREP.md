## Concept brief (1A)

### One-line promise

**VERVE SOCIAL** is a full-viewport **creator social platform hero**: plum-ink night, hot rose + amber signal, Clash Display lockup, a living culture film of real human presence, and an infinite marquee of community energy — free-play film, never scroll-scrubbed.

### Who + desire

- Social / creator / community apps and “new network” startups  
- Want **presence and belonging**, not another grey SaaS dashboard hero  
- Sell the *vibe of being there* before the feed product details  

### Signature behavior (plain English)

1. Lifestyle film free-plays muted full-bleed.  
2. Type + CTAs stagger in (Framer).  
3. Infinite horizontal marquee of handles / soft social chips (not real competitor brands).  
4. Desktop only: soft film wrap scale parallax on scroll (1 → ~1.06), **never** `video.currentTime` scrub.  
5. Reduced motion: poster still + static marquee row + full type.  

### Film subject law

| Required | Forbidden |
|----------|-----------|
| Human presence / social culture energy (faces OK as lifestyle, not influencers holding logo phones as ads) | Readable app UI, feed chrome, stickers, captions, hashtags burned in frame |
| Warm night + golden-hour practicals; plum shadows, rose/amber rim | Neon cyberpunk city as hero world |
| Large dark voids / soft bokeh for type legibility | Solid white flash frames; pure stock “hands on laptop SaaS” |
| Seamless loopable ~20–30s silent 1080p+ | Watermarks, brand logos, competitor names, text |
