# Nomad Travel - generate a new background film

**Role:** Client background film only (pure world, no website UI, no text, no logos).  
**Shipped pack already includes** a ready film in `assets/nomad-montage-v1.mp4` plus poster. Use this file only when you want a **new** world.

---

## What good looks like

A seamless cinematic travel montage of **extraordinary empty places** - cliff villas, desert lodges, hidden infinity edges - warm terracotta and cream grade, zero people as heroes, zero UI. The aspiration a private travel club sells.

| Spec | Target |
|------|--------|
| Aspect | 16:9 |
| Resolution | 1920x1080 minimum · 4K preferred |
| Length | ~12-30s loop-friendly (silent) |
| Motion | Slow aerial or elevated glide, locked horizon |
| Audio | Silent |

Copy everything inside the fence into Runway, Kling, Luma, Veo, Grok Imagine, or similar.

```
Cinematic ultra-premium 4K seamless loop, 14 to 30 seconds, 24fps film feel, no audio.

SUBJECT: Empty luxury travel destinations from a continuous high-end cinema language - one coherent world family (not a random stock mashup). Prefer ONE continuous take or soft invisible morphs between 2-3 related vistas of the same geographic mood:

Primary world: Slow aerial / elevated glide over a cliffside luxury villa and infinity pool at golden hour - warm terracotta stone, cream limestone, deep espresso shadows in rock faces, turquoise or ink-blue water far below, soft haze, Mediterranean or arid coastal paradise. Pool water barely ripples. No people, no boats as heroes, no readable hotel brand marks, no UI.

Alt A: Slow drone push over an empty desert boutique lodge at magic hour - long shadows, terracotta dunes, a single glass pavilion catching amber light, heat shimmer, absolute solitude.

Alt B: Slow glide through misty jungle canopy toward a hidden infinity edge overlooking emerald valleys - warm wet greens graded with terracotta highlights, private-house luxury, not backpacker trail.

CAMERA: Continuous slow aerial or elevated dolly, locked horizon, high-end cinema language, shallow depth of field mid-distance, subtle anamorphic character, one smooth move only - no cuts, no whip pans, no handheld chaos, no snap zooms, no FPV freestyle.

MOTION ENERGY: Contemplative prestige travel pace. Light crawls. Water breathes. Haze drifts. Perfect seamless loop: end frame compositionally matches start so the glide can repeat forever.

LOOK / GRADE: Warm luxury editorial grade - espresso voids, terracotta highlights on stone and dust, cream in sun-kissed limestone and sky glow, controlled bloom, gentle film grain. Aman resort brand film meets Conde Nast Traveler opening shot. Not teal-orange blockbuster cliche, not neon cyberpunk, not cold blue fintech chrome, not pure white wellness spa.

MOOD: Quiet wealth of a private travel club. Expensive, warm, unhurried, sovereign.

ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN:
- People, faces, hands, tourists, staff, models as focus
- Readable text, logos, watermarks, hotel brands, UI, HUD, captions, subtitles, map pins
- Neon cyan/pink cyberpunk city
- Cheap hostel / backpacker energy
- Overcrowded tourist beaches as hero subject
- Camera shake, jump cuts, 3D text titles, brand marks

TECH: 16:9, 3840x2160 preferred (or 1920x1080 minimum), seamless loop, silent, photoreal cinematic.
```
